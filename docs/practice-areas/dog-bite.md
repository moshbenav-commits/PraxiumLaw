# Module: Dog Bite / Animal Attack

Animal-attack injuries — high-frequency, fast-cycle, near-PI-core cases. Ships with [premises liability](./premises-liability.md) in wave 1.

## 1. Scope
In: dog bites and other animal attacks on people. Out→refer: attacks on livestock/pets (property claims), dangerous-dog administrative defense.

## 2. Intake deltas
Owner identity + relationship to victim (family-defendant sensitivity), attack location (owner's property → homeowner's policy; elsewhere → renter's/umbrella), animal-control report filed?, prior-bite/aggression knowledge, photos of wounds **now** (wound progression series is the damages engine), rabies/vaccination verification, child victim → [minors' compromise](../pi-case-os/articles/30-minors-compromise-and-court-approval.md) overlay.

## 3. Lifecycle deltas
Adds `animal_control_report` retrieval task and a **wound-photo cadence** (day 1/7/30/scar-maturity) to the treatment phase. Strict-liability vs. one-bite jurisdictions change the liability workup checklist, not the lifecycle.

## 4. Document pack
Animal-control records request, homeowner's/renter's tender letter, prior-incident inquiry, scarring-evaluation referral letter (plastic surgery consult), demand with photo-series exhibit block.

## 5. Deadline pack (categories)
General PI SOL · minor-victim tolling · public-entity notice if government-owned animal/facility.

## 6. Damages model deltas
Adds scarring/disfigurement valuation (photo series + plastic-surgeon future-revision estimates) and psychological-injury track (especially child victims — counseling records).

## 7. Counterparty map
Homeowner's/renter's carriers — different adjuster desks than auto; policies often have animal exclusions (coverage check is step one). Family-defendant dynamics: client may resist suing a relative — **the claim targets the policy**; client-comms templates handle this framing.

## 8. Compliance flags
Standard PI set; heightened sensitivity in family-defendant comms.

## 9. KPIs
Coverage-identified latency, photo-cadence compliance, resolution value vs. scarring grade — plus core PI KPIs.

## 10. Reuse map
~95% unchanged from PI core. New content only: intake set, photo-cadence task pack, homeowner's tender templates.
