# Module: Premises Liability

Slip/trip-and-fall, negligent security, dangerous conditions on property. Smallest delta from the PI core — first module to ship. Extends article [41](../pi-case-os/articles/41-premises-liability-slip-and-fall.md).

## 1. Scope
In: injuries on commercial/residential/public property from dangerous conditions or inadequate security. Out→refer: workplace falls (route to [workers' comp](./workers-compensation.md) first).

## 2. Intake deltas
Exact location + property owner/occupier (often unknown at intake — ownership lookup task), condition that caused the fall, **incident report filed? with whom?**, footwear/weather, witnesses, whether store offered aid or statements were given. Urgency triggers: **CCTV retention windows (days, not weeks)** — preservation letter is a day-one automatic; public-entity defendant → tort-claim notice clock (very short).

## 3. Lifecycle deltas
Adds `evidence_preservation` sub-phase at case open (spoliation letters out within 24–48h, gate on proof-of-delivery). Adds `ownership_and_insurer_identification` before claims open (registered-agent + property-records lookup). Otherwise the PI lifecycle applies unchanged.

## 4. Document pack
Preservation/spoliation letters (CCTV, incident reports, maintenance/sweep logs), tort-claim notice (public entities), ownership-inquiry letters, premises-specific demand sections (notice/constructive-notice evidence block).

## 5. Deadline pack (categories)
General PI SOL · **public-entity tort-claim notice window** · recreational-use and other immunity-related notice rules.

## 6. Damages model deltas
None — PI worksheet applies. Comparative-fault exposure is higher; the case-grade model weights liability evidence more heavily.

## 7. Counterparty map
CGL carriers and self-insured retailers' TPAs; negligent-security claims meet excess layers. Same reduction/negotiation rails as PI.

## 8. Compliance flags
Standard PI set. No investigator contact with represented property employees.

## 9. KPIs
Preservation-letter latency (open→sent), ownership-identified latency, notice-deadline incidents (target zero) — plus core PI KPIs.

## 10. Reuse map
~95% unchanged. New content only: preservation letter pack, public-entity notice pack, intake question set.
