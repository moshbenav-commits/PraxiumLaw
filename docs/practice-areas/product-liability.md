# Module: Product Liability

Injuries from defective products (design, manufacturing, warning defects). Ships with [med-mal](./medical-malpractice.md) — shares the expert-management core build.

## 1. Scope
In: consumer/industrial product injuries, vehicle defect (crashworthiness) claims. Out→refer: pure economic-loss product claims (warranty/commercial). Overlaps: a defective-product injury inside a mass filing wave routes to [mass tort](./mass-tort.md).

## 2. Intake deltas
Product make/model/serial/lot, purchase source and date, **where the product is right now**, how it failed, other incidents known, packaging/manuals retained?, modification/misuse questions. Urgency trigger: **the product itself is the case — secure it before anyone discards or repairs it.** Day-one: product-preservation protocol + spoliation letters to any party holding it (repair shop, insurer, employer).

## 3. Lifecycle deltas
Adds `product_secured` gate at open (chain-of-custody record, `CORE-BUILD: evidence-custody log`, shared with future criminal-adjacent needs). Adds `defect_theory_screening` (expert review; recall/complaint-database sweep — CPSC/NHTSA-class public sources) before demand. Litigation-heavy like med-mal.

## 4. Document pack
Preservation/spoliation letters, chain-of-custody forms, recall/complaint research memo shell, notice letters to manufacturer/distributor/retailer chain, expert engagement set, demand with defect-theory exhibit structure.

## 5. Deadline pack (categories)
PI SOL · **statute of repose for products** (absolute bar from sale/manufacture date) · warranty-notice windows where breach claims are joined.

## 6. Damages model deltas
PI worksheet plus punitive-exposure assessment (notice-to-manufacturer evidence) — flagged for attorney evaluation only.

## 7. Counterparty map
Manufacturers' products carriers + national coordinating defense counsel; retailers/distributors tender up the chain. Negotiation is slower, litigation-anchored; early resolution usually only after defect theory survives expert scrutiny.

## 8. Compliance flags
Never test/alter the product without joint-inspection protocol (spoliation kills the case both ways). Expert fees hourly only. Public-database findings are leads, not evidence — no overstatement in demands.

## 9. KPIs
Product-secured latency (target: <72h from signup), screen-to-accept ratio, expert-cost per case vs. budget, repose-deadline incidents (target zero).

## 10. Reuse map
Unchanged: intake rail, comms, scheduling, billing/lien/disbursement, closeout. Shared `CORE-BUILD`s with med-mal: expert-witness management; new: evidence-custody log.
