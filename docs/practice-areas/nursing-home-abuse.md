# Module: Nursing Home Abuse / Neglect

Injuries to residents of long-term-care facilities: falls, pressure injuries, malnutrition/dehydration, medication errors, physical abuse, elopement. Sits between premises and med-mal in complexity.

## 1. Scope
In: claims against skilled-nursing, assisted-living, memory-care facilities. Out→refer: adult-guardianship disputes, criminal prosecution (report and refer — see compliance).

## 2. Intake deltas
Caller is usually **family, not the injured person** — authority check first (POA/guardianship/next-of-kin; deceased resident → [wrongful death overlay](./wrongful-death.md)). Facility name/chain, admission date, care-plan complaints history, state-survey complaints filed?, photos of injuries/conditions, roommate/staff witnesses. Urgency triggers: resident still in facility and at risk (**safety first — care escalation before case workup**), facility chart amendment risk (records request day-one), arbitration-agreement signed at admission?

## 3. Lifecycle deltas
Adds `authority_established` gate before signup. Adds `regulatory_records` workup phase (state survey/inspection history, staffing data — public sources). Adds `arbitration_screen` (admission-packet arbitration clause changes the forum — attorney gate on strategy). Med-mal-style expert screening applies when the theory is care-standard violation.

## 4. Document pack
Authority verification set (POA/letters), facility chart request (full chart incl. care plans, MAR, incident reports, staffing schedules), preservation letters, state-agency complaint shells, arbitration-clause analysis memo shell, demand with regulatory-history exhibit block.

## 5. Deadline pack (categories)
Med-mal vs. general-negligence SOL characterization (state-dependent, changes the clock) · survival/wrongful-death statutes · pre-suit notice where facilities are covered by med-mal acts · arbitration-demand windows.

## 6. Damages model deltas
Adds dignity/abuse non-economic framing, survival-action damages, and **regulatory-violation evidence weighting** in case grade; punitive exposure flag for chain-level understaffing patterns (attorney evaluation only).

## 7. Counterparty map
LTC carriers and facility-chain captives; corporate defendants layer management companies — entity-mapping task to find the real defendant. Arbitration forum common.

## 8. Compliance flags
**Mandatory elder-abuse reporting** duties may apply to what intake learns — per-state protocol, attorney-owned. Client capacity/guardianship ethics. HIPAA at maximum sensitivity.

## 9. KPIs
Authority-established latency, chart-request latency (target: day one), regulatory-records completion rate, arbitration-screen completion before strategy gate — plus core KPIs.

## 10. Reuse map
Unchanged: comms, scheduling, billing/liens, trust, closeout. Reuses med-mal expert screening. New content: authority packet, regulatory-records playbook, arbitration screen.
