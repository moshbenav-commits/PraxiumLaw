# Transfer to Litigation Notices

**Category:** Litigation handoff  
**Source:** Training PI — Transfer to Litigation Letter  
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).

---

## Purpose

When a case moves to litigation (or a new litigating attorney), notify **medical providers and insurance companies** so correspondence routes correctly.

## When to send

If the case is in a **litigation phase** and you receive provider bills/records or carrier correspondence, send the transfer letter (do not leave them writing to the pre-lit team only).

## Contents

- Litigating attorney name and contact details  
- Client name and matter identifiers  
- Send method (fax/email/mail)  
- Staff initials  

## After send

Note on the task that the transfer letter was sent; attach the PDF; complete the task.

## Product rule

On phase change to lit, queue transfer letters for all active providers and open carriers. Require litigating attorney contact fields before send.
