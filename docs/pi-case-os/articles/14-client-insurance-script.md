# Client Script: Property Damage Only With Insurers

**Category:** Insurance Communication Rules · Scripts  
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.  
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).  
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md)  
**Audience:** Case managers coaching clients

---

Clients will get calls from insurance adjusters. Training requires a bright line.

## The rule

The client may discuss **property damage only** with insurers. They must not discuss injuries, treatment, or how the accident happened.

## Script to give the client

> I am only authorized to speak with you about property damage. If you want to talk about injuries or the accident, you need to speak with my attorney.

## Why it matters

Recorded statements about fault, speed, symptoms, or prior injuries become exhibits against value. Property damage logistics (inspection location, rental, total loss) can proceed without opening the injury narrative.

## Pair with the text line

Remind clients that the case **text line is text-only** — calling it will not reach staff and creates frustration. Photos of the vehicles should be texted to that line and renamed in the file as **client** vs **defendant** images.
