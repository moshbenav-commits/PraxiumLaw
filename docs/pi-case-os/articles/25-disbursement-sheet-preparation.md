# Disbursement Sheet Preparation

**Category:** Settlement & Disbursement
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md)

---

## Purpose

The disbursement sheet reconciles everything owed out of a settlement — medical balances, liens/subrogation, case expenses, loans, fees, and the client's net — so the case can move to reduction requests and payout without shorting any party. Build it only when a final settlement offer is in hand.

## Part 1 — Medical bills

- Confirm every provider in the medical-bills tab has matching bills **and** records; flag any record with no corresponding bill.
- Reconcile the file server / document store against the case file so no bill is missing.
- Enter each provider's **original billed amount**; track insurance payments, write-offs, and client out-of-pocket separately so the correct **balance** flows through.
- No reduction request should go out on an incomplete billed total.

## Part 2 — Health subrogation liens

- Check the liens and health-insurance sections for any Notice of Lien or subrogation claim.
- **Verify relatedness:** each charge on the lien should match crash-related treatment (typically hospital, ambulance, ER physician, ER radiology on the date of loss). Dates of service before the accident, or unrelated later dates, should be disputed and the lienholder asked to amend.
- Enter the amount the health plan actually **paid** (its recovery interest), not the billed amount, and mark which provider charges are captured by the lien so balances don't double-count. See [`29-health-insurance-subrogation.md`](29-health-insurance-subrogation.md).

## Part 3 — Expenses & loans

- Account for every firm-advanced cost: records/copy fees, police reports, policy-limit investigations, filing fees, service, postage, experts.
- **Pre-settlement loans / litigation funding:** compute principal **plus** accrued interest per the funding agreement and enter as a separate line — these must be repaid at disbursement.
- Split shared costs (e.g., a policy-limit investigation covering related cases) across the cases and note the math.

## Close-out

- Add a dated note at the top of the sheet describing what you updated, and your initials.
- Record any discrepancy (record without a bill, unpaid invoice, report without a receipt) and task the responsible person.
- Route to the case manager for the reduction percentage (if reducing) or mark ready for disbursement.

## Product rule / attorney gate

Reduction percentages, fee adjustments, and final disbursement figures require **attorney or case-manager approval** before anything is sent or paid. The system should lock the sheet for review and require the approver's role before generating reduction or disbursement letters.
