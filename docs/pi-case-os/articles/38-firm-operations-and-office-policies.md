## Purpose

A starting framework for the operational side of a small PI firm: roles, onboarding, and baseline office policy. Employment law is **highly state-specific** — have HR/employment counsel review any policy before adopting it.

## Roles & duties

Define who does what so nothing falls between seats. See the [`../training-guides/`](../training-guides/) for role-specific guides (intake specialist, case manager, paralegal, billing/disbursements, supervising attorney, firm admin, support/clerical, virtual assistant). Keep a simple duties matrix mapping each lifecycle phase to an owner.

## Onboarding

- Standard onboarding checklist: system access and role-based permissions, security/confidentiality training, template and SOP orientation, and the [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md) acknowledgment.
- Grant least-privilege access; sensitive actions (trust disbursement, demand send) stay gated to the right roles.

## Payroll & HR (framework only)

- Classify workers (employee vs. contractor) and handle payroll, overtime, and benefits per **federal and your state's** labor law — verify with counsel; do not copy another state's practice.
- Maintain confidentiality and data-security policies covering client PII and health information (HIPAA-adjacent handling of medical records).

## Office policy baseline

- Attendance/availability expectations, especially for client-facing response times.
- Communication and file-hygiene standards (see [`34-correspondence-and-communications.md`](34-correspondence-and-communications.md)) — everything to the file.
- Escalation paths: when staff must involve a supervising attorney (coverage problems, drops, deadline risk, client complaints).
- Document retention and destruction schedule consistent with ethics rules (client files and trust records — commonly ≥5 years post-matter; verify).

## Attorney / firm gate

Employment, payroll, benefits, and retention policies must be **reviewed by qualified counsel** for your jurisdiction. This framework is informational and not employment-law advice.
