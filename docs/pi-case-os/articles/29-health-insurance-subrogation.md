# Health Insurance Subrogation & Medical Liens

**Category:** Settlement & Disbursement

**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md) — Medicare MSP/CMS; ERISA & the made-whole doctrine

## Purpose

When a health plan (private, ERISA, Medicare, or Medicaid) pays for accident-related treatment, it generally has a right to be reimbursed out of the client's recovery. Identify these interests **early**, verify them, and resolve them before disbursement — missing one can expose the client and the firm.

## Identify the interest early

- At intake and again before demand, ask what paid for treatment: private health insurance, an employer/ERISA plan, Medicare, Medicaid, MedPay/PIP, or a workers'-comp carrier.
- Open the corresponding subrogation/lien claim as soon as you know, so the final payoff isn't a surprise at settlement.

## Types of interest (each behaves differently — verify)

- **Private / state-law plans:** rights depend on the policy and on state law (including any state anti-subrogation or made-whole rules).
- **ERISA (self-funded employer plans):** governed by federal law. The **made-whole** doctrine is only a *default* — clear plan language can override it (*US Airways v. McCutchen*). Read the plan document; get the summary plan description and the reimbursement provision.
- **Medicare (Secondary Payer):** conditional payments must be repaid. Work the recovery through CMS/BCRC and the **MSPRP** portal; get the **final** conditional-payment/demand amount. Note CMS reporting obligations and low-dollar thresholds — verify current figures on cms.gov.
- **Medicaid:** state-agency recovery, often with federal anti-lien limits on portions of recovery — check your state's rules.

## Verify relatedness (the core task)

- Compare each charge on the lien against the crash-related treatment in the file. Same-date-of-loss hospital/ambulance/ER charges are usually related; pre-accident dates are not.
- **Dispute unrelated charges** and ask the plan to amend the lien.
- For related charges you have no bills/records for, verify with the client and request the records before paying.
- If related hospital charges belong on the plan's lien but aren't there, coordinate before finalizing.

## Reduce and resolve

- Ask for reductions where allowed (procurement-cost / attorney-fee share, made-whole arguments, hardship). Document the basis.
- Enter the **final** verified payoff on the [disbursement sheet](25-disbursement-sheet-preparation.md) and pay it at [disbursement](27-settlement-and-disbursement-workflow.md).

## Attorney gate

Lien negotiation strategy, made-whole/relatedness disputes, and final payoffs are **attorney-supervised**. Do not release settlement funds until government and plan liens are resolved or properly reserved.
