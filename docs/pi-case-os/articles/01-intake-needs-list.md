# The Intake Needs List: What Every New PI Client Must Bring

**Category:** Client Intake  
**Source:** Personal Injury Workflow training transcript  
**Audience:** Case managers, intake staff

---

When a new motor-vehicle client signs, the case does not start with a demand letter. It starts with a **Needs List** — the homework packet that keeps the file from stalling weeks later.

## What happens at intake

While the client fills out the MVA questionnaire, staff copy whatever documents the client already has. The case manager reviews the questionnaire for legibility, opens the project in the case system, and prints releases. The Needs List is edited live: check off what you already received, highlight what is still missing, and hand the client a clean copy to take home.

## Core documents on the Needs List

Training materials list these items:

- Insurance **declaration page** (not only the insurance card)
- Hospital bills and records
- Ambulance bills
- Any other medical bills already in hand
- Driver’s license / identification
- Health insurance card
- Social Security information (as required for forms)
- Vehicle registration (optional)
- Accident exchange ticket / defendant information
- Body shop or tow-yard information
- Vehicle repair estimate (often obtained later)

## Declaration page tip

If the client does not have declarations, they can call their agent and ask for the declaration page **without announcing that they were in an accident**. The goal is coverage detail (MedPay, rental, collision, UM/UIM), not a recorded claim statement on day one.

## Make it actionable

Write provider appointments on the Needs List: pain management or clinic name, chiro or PT name, address, phone, date, and time. Add the referral body shop the same way. Clients lose papers; a single sheet with appointments and missing documents reduces no-shows and gaps.

## Follow up in three to seven days

Clients go home and forget. Training directs a call within about **three to seven days** (and a structured **three-day** task in the case system) to ask:

- Did you get the declaration page?
- Did you get the car looked at for an estimate?
- Did any hospital or ambulance bills arrive in the mail?
- Can you text a photo of your health insurance card?
- Did you attend your doctor and chiro appointments?

The Needs List is not paperwork theater. Missing items on day one become missing exhibits on demand day.
