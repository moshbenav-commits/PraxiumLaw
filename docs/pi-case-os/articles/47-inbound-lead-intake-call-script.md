# Inbound Lead Intake Call Script

**Category:** Intake / Business development
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.
**White-label:** Any firm. Replace `{{FIRM_NAME}}` etc. **Put this in your firm's voice.** See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md)

---

## Purpose

A consistent, warm way to handle an **inbound** call from someone who was hurt and reached out (or was referred) — not cold solicitation. It captures the facts the firm needs, sets expectations, and books the sign-up, **without giving legal advice or promising outcomes**. This is the phone-greeting companion to [lead intake ops](35-lead-intake-and-signup-operations.md).

> **Compliance:** this is for **inbound** callers who contacted the firm. Do not use it to solicit accident victims — see [referral & marketing compliance](44-referral-sources-and-marketing-compliance.md). Intake staff **do not give legal advice or quote case value.**

## Call flow

1. **Warm open + empathy** — acknowledge what happened; confirm they're safe.
2. **Listen** — let them tell the story before you start collecting fields.
3. **Gather the facts** (qualification checklist below).
4. **Set expectations** — what happens next; no promises.
5. **Book the sign-up** — schedule the consult; tell them what to bring.
6. **Handoff** — create the lead, run a [conflict check](13-conflict-check-and-related-cases.md), route to the attorney for acceptance.

## Qualification checklist (capture while you talk)

- [ ] Name, best phone, email, preferred language
- [ ] **Date of loss** → flag the [statute of limitations](../REFERENCES.md) immediately if it's near
- [ ] What happened / where (facts of loss); type (auto, [premises](41-premises-liability-slip-and-fall.md), etc.)
- [ ] **Were you working when it happened?** (→ [workers' comp](42-workers-compensation-coordination.md) flag)
- [ ] Injuries; treated yet? where? still treating?
- [ ] Police/incident report? number?
- [ ] Other party & **insurance**; their own coverage (UM/UIM, MedPay/PIP) — [coverage reference](33-auto-insurance-coverage-reference.md)
- [ ] Prior accidents/claims; any prior attorney on this matter
- [ ] How they heard about the firm (source tracking)

## Sample script (adapt to your voice)

> **Open:** "Thank you for calling {{FIRM_NAME}}, this is [name]. I'm so sorry to hear you were in an accident — first, are you okay right now? … I'm glad you reached out. I'm going to ask a few questions so we can understand what happened and how we can help."
>
> **Facts:** "Can you walk me through what happened, in your own words? … And when was this? … Where did it happen? … Were you working at the time?"
>
> **Injuries/treatment:** "How were you hurt? … Have you seen a doctor or been to the ER yet? … Are you still in treatment?"
>
> **Insurance/report:** "Do you know the other driver's insurance? … Do you have your own auto coverage? … Was a police report made?"
>
> **Expectations (no advice, no promises):** "Here's what happens next: I'll set you up with our team to review everything. I can't give legal advice or promise a result on this call, but we'll go over your options when we meet."
>
> **Book it:** "Let's schedule your consultation. Please bring [needs-list items]. Would [time] work?"
>
> **Close:** "You're in good hands — we'll take it from here. If anything changes or you have questions, here's how to reach us."

## Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Lead with empathy; listen first | Sound like a script robot |
| Capture facts accurately | **Give legal advice** or interpret the law |
| Say "we'll review your options" | **Promise money, value, or a win** |
| Flag SOL/urgent items to the attorney | Pressure or rush the caller |
| Note the referral source | Discuss fees beyond your firm's approved talking points |

## Handoff

Create the lead in the [case-management system](36-case-management-system-setup.md) with all captured fields, attach notes, run the conflict check, and route to the attorney for the **accept/decline** decision. On acceptance, send the [intake packet](23-intake-forms-and-signature-packet.md) and a completed [needs list](01-intake-needs-list.md).

## Attorney gate

Case acceptance and any fee discussion are **attorney decisions**. Intake staff qualify and schedule; they do not advise or guarantee.
