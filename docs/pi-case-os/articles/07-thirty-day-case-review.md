# How to Run a 30-Day (and 5-Week) Case Review

**Category:** Treatment Milestone Checklist  
**Source:** 5-Week and 30-Day Case Review — Pain Management and MRI  
**Audience:** Case managers

---

## Purpose

The **30-day case review** (and related five-week checkpoint) forces a structured look at whether the medical story is developing — or stalling.

## What to review

Prioritize:

- Pain management records
- Neurologist studies (for example EMGs)
- MRI reports

Deprioritize routine chiropractic notes unless something exceptional appears.

## MRI block

1. Open the MRI report
2. Confirm it is an MRI, not an X-ray
3. Quote the interpretation (levels, measurements, impressions)
4. Attach the MRI to the case-review fields
5. Summarize in the review notes

## Pain management block

1. Attach recent PM records
2. Note reported pain intensity ranges
3. Read the **plan** section for injections, referrals, or surgery language
4. Flag anything outstanding for the attorney

## Escalation

If there is no MRI by about eight weeks, call the pain doctor. Training ties missing imaging directly to lower settlements and client dissatisfaction later.
