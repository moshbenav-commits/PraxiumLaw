# Redaction Checklist Before Sending Records to Carriers

**Category:** Insurance Communication · Records  
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.  
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md)  
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).

---

## Why redact

Carriers use personal identifiers to search for **prior accidents and injuries** and reduce offers. Incomplete redaction can force litigation, delay payment, and raise costs.

## Always redact (training list)

- Date of birth  
- Age  
- Address  
- Social Security number  
- Medicare ID  
- VIN  
- License plate  
- Health insurance identifiers  
- Sensitive health information (e.g. STDs, HIV)  
- Ethnicity  
- Lien holder information (when sending to adverse carriers)

## Apply to

- Police reports  
- Medical bills (e.g. HICF)  
- Medical records  
- Repair estimates  

## Product rule

PraxiumLaw should block “send to carrier” exports of medical/PD packages until a **redaction checklist** is acknowledged (or automated redaction is applied). Counsel should confirm jurisdiction-specific privacy rules.
