# Lead Intake & Sign-Up Operations

**Category:** Intake / Business development
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md) — advertising & solicitation ethics rules

---

## Purpose

Turn inbound leads into signed, qualified clients quickly and ethically, and hand a clean file to case management. Speed-to-lead and disciplined qualification drive a PI practice; sloppy intake creates unwinnable or unprofitable files.

## Handle the lead

- **Respond fast.** Contact new leads as quickly as possible; log the source (referral, campaign, web) for ROI tracking.
- **Empathy first, facts second.** Acknowledge the person's situation, then gather the qualifying facts. Keep a light script for consistency — but never let a script pressure someone or promise an outcome.
- **Ethics:** follow your state's rules on advertising, solicitation, and referral/lead-source arrangements. Do not pay for or accept leads in a way your jurisdiction prohibits, and disclose any required advertising language.

## Qualify before signing

Capture and screen against your firm's criteria:

- Date of loss vs. **statute of limitations** (see [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md)); flag anything near the deadline immediately.
- Liability picture and available **coverage** (3P limits, UM/UIM, MedPay/PIP — see [`33-auto-insurance-coverage-reference.md`](33-auto-insurance-coverage-reference.md)).
- Injuries and treatment status; prior accidents/claims.
- **Conflict check** before sign-up ([`13-conflict-check-and-related-cases.md`](13-conflict-check-and-related-cases.md)).

## Sign-up

- Send the retainer / fee agreement, HIPAA authorization, and intake forms; contingency-fee agreements must be **in writing** (Model Rule 1.5 — see references).
- Confirm the client understands the fee, costs, and process.
- Open the matter, create the file, and hand off to case management with a completed [intake needs list](01-intake-needs-list.md) and [signature packet](23-intake-forms-and-signature-packet.md).

## Attorney gate

Case acceptance/decline and any fee terms are **attorney decisions**. Intake staff qualify and recommend; they do not give legal advice or guarantee results.
