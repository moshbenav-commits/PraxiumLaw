# Settlement & Disbursement Workflow

**Category:** Settlement & Disbursement
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md) — trust accounting (Model Rule 1.15), Medicare/ERISA liens

---

## Purpose

The end-to-end path from a final settlement offer to paying providers, satisfying liens, and delivering the client's check — with trust-accounting discipline throughout.

## Phase 1 — Lock the case before releasing

- Stop the treatment clock: cancel open appointments so no new bills accrue.
- Confirm coverage facts that change the math — e.g., whether a UM/UIM waiver was signed (an unsigned waiver may default the client to statutory minimum UM/UIM in some states).
- On a full-limits offer, obtain whatever your jurisdiction/carrier requires to close (e.g., an affidavit of no additional assets/insurance, or a signed hold-harmless where the affidavit can't be obtained). **Attorney reviews any release before the client signs.**
- **Do not sign any release until the entire case is resolved, including property damage.** Watch for "all claims" language that could inadvertently release a first-party (UM/UIM) claim or additional parties (driver, owner, carrier).
- Obtain the **final** health-insurance/Medicare lien figure (Medicare typically must say "final").

## Phase 2 — Verify only what needs verifying

Re-verify balances that can still move — surgery centers, anesthesia, pain management, ongoing specialists, and any provider the client returned to. Balances already final (completed chiro/PT, prior-verified imaging) generally don't need re-verification. This is an efficiency practice, not a rule — when in doubt, verify.

## Phase 3 — Reductions & the disbursement sheet

- Finalize the [disbursement sheet](25-disbursement-sheet-preparation.md); include **all** liens and loans: health-insurance/subrogation, workers'-comp, child-support, and funding loans. Don't forget MedPay/PIP credits.
- Send [reduction requests](26-reduction-requests-and-negotiation.md); apply approvals to the sheet.

## Phase 4 — Trust handling & checks

- Deposit the settlement into the **client trust account**; keep client funds separate from firm funds and disburse only against cleared funds (see Model Rule 1.15 in [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md)).
- Record every check in the trust ledger and reconcile.
- Prepare disbursement letters to each provider/lienholder identifying the payee, amount, and payment address, with a receipt to sign and return.

## Phase 5 — Client sign-off & payout

Before releasing the client's check, collect the documents your jurisdiction/carrier requires — commonly a signed release, a signed disbursement/settlement statement, any required government-benefit (e.g., Medicare/CMS) form, and a hold-harmless where applicable. Upload the signed statement to the file, mail provider payments with their letters, complete file KPIs, move the case to reconciliation, and deposit the firm's earned fee.

## Attorney gate

Settlement authority, release approval, reduction percentages, and trust disbursement remain under **licensed-attorney control**.
