# Mass Tort Case Intake & Management

**Category:** Department — Mass Torts
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md)

---

## Purpose

Mass torts (defective drugs/devices, product liability, environmental/exposure campaigns) run on a **different operating model** than single-event auto PI: high intake volume, strict product/exposure and injury criteria, proof-of-use documentation, and often co-counsel or an MDL. This article frames the department; specifics depend on the active litigation and your co-counsel agreements.

## Intake & qualification

- Maintain a **per-campaign criteria sheet**: qualifying product/exposure, dates of use, qualifying injuries/diagnoses, and disqualifiers. Screen every lead against it before signing.
- Capture **proof of use/exposure** early (pharmacy records, purchase records, implant/device cards, employment/exposure history) — cases fail on missing proof.
- Use campaign-specific retainers and disclosures; confirm any **fee-sharing / co-counsel** terms are in writing and client-disclosed per your ethics rules.

## Case management

- Track each client against the litigation's **census/registry** requirements and deadlines (fact sheets, plaintiff profile forms, records collection).
- Records-heavy workflow: standardized medical and product-use record requests, indexed per client.
- Watch **statutes of limitations and repose** and any registration/tolling orders specific to the litigation.
- Keep clients updated on the realistic (often long) timeline; set expectations up front.

## Settlement / resolution

- Mass-tort resolutions often run through a **settlement grid / point system** administered by a claims administrator rather than individual negotiation.
- Lien resolution (Medicare/Medicaid/ERISA — see [`29-health-insurance-subrogation.md`](29-health-insurance-subrogation.md)) is frequently handled through a coordinated lien-resolution administrator; verify each client's payoff before disbursement.

## Attorney gate

Campaign selection, qualification decisions, co-counsel arrangements, and settlement participation are **attorney decisions**. This department should not sign or advance clients on a campaign the supervising attorney has not approved.
