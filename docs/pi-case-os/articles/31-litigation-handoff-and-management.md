# Litigation Handoff & Case Management

**Category:** Litigation
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md) — statutes of limitations

---

## Purpose

Move a case cleanly from pre-litigation to suit when demand negotiations stall, limits are contested, liability is disputed, or the statute of limitations is approaching — without dropping deadlines or breaking provider/carrier communication.

## Triggers to consider litigation

- Demand stalled or lowball offer; disputed liability; contested coverage/limits.
- **Statute of limitations approaching** — the hard gate. Calendar the specific state/claim-type deadline (see references) and any minor-tolling; escalate well before it runs.

## Handoff checklist (pre-lit → litigating attorney)

- [ ] Confirm and re-calendar the SOL and all sub-deadlines.
- [ ] Complete demand package: all bills/records reconciled, imaging, injections/surgery, prior-records requests resolved.
- [ ] Coverage summary: liability limits, UM/UIM, MedPay/PIP, other applicable policies.
- [ ] Lien/subrogation status current (health, Medicare/Medicaid, workers'-comp, funding).
- [ ] Client contact current; set expectations about the litigation timeline.
- [ ] Route open provider bills and carrier correspondence to the litigating attorney — see [`22-transfer-to-litigation.md`](22-transfer-to-litigation.md) for the transfer-notice letters.

## Litigation phase — case management hygiene

- Maintain a litigation calendar: complaint/answer, discovery, disclosures, depositions, mediation, trial.
- Keep the file's coverage, liens, and medical specials updated as treatment or balances change; send transfer notices to any new providers.
- Preserve evidence and continue social-media/sub-rosa awareness ([`06-social-media-and-subrosa.md`](06-social-media-and-subrosa.md)).
- On resolution (verdict, settlement, or minor's compromise), return to the [settlement & disbursement workflow](27-settlement-and-disbursement-workflow.md).

## Attorney gate

Filing suit, pleadings, discovery strategy, and settlement authority are **attorney functions**. Staff support the calendar, file, and correspondence under supervision.
