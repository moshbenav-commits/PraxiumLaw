# Demand Package Preparation Checklist

**Category:** Demand Package Preparation  
**Source:** Demand Prep Part 1; PI Workflow; demand gather-records modules  
**Audience:** Case managers  
**Gate:** Attorney or office manager reviews demand before send

---

## Before you draft

Demand work fails when early steps were skipped. Training’s recurring lesson: if Meds was never maintained, demand day is archaeology.

### Checklist

- [ ] Every Meds provider has matching bills and/or records (or a documented futures estimate)
- [ ] Hospital, ER physician, radiology, and ambulance are complete when ER care occurred
- [ ] MRI reports attached and summarized
- [ ] Injection/surgical notices and FE amounts reconciled
- [ ] Lien verifications sent where balances are uncertain
- [ ] Loans and other liens entered (they must be repaid at disbursement)
- [ ] Out-of-pocket expenses on the Expenses tab
- [ ] Police report in file (if one exists)
- [ ] Adjuster / claim numbers current from acknowledgments
- [ ] Prior accident records requested when priors exist (last five years — records only, not bills)

## Request discipline

When something is missing:

1. Task records staff with a **specific** provider (never “everyone”)
2. Task **yourself** to follow up in a week
3. Do not assume a completed task means documents arrived

## Review and send

- Draft the demand (and UIM demand when applicable)
- **Attorney or office manager reviews**
- Send and **calendar the response**
- Keep the client on at least **monthly evaluations** while the demand is outstanding

## Med totals vs limits (rule of thumb from training)

One worked example: with $100k per person, $25k UM/UIM, and $5k MedPay, staff aimed for roughly **$60k–$80k** in medical specials before starting demand. This is an operational heuristic from training, not a valuation formula.
