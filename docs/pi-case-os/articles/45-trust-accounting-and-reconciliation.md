# Trust Accounting & Three-Way Reconciliation

**Category:** Settlement & Disbursement / Compliance
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md) — Model Rule 1.15 (safekeeping property)

---

## Purpose

Client settlement money is not the firm's money. Mishandling it is one of the fastest ways to face discipline or disbarment. This SOP defines how the firm holds, tracks, and reconciles client funds. **Trust-accounting rules are state-specific** — confirm your state bar's exact requirements; this is the general framework under Model Rule 1.15.

## Core rules (non-negotiable)

- **Segregation:** client/third-party funds go in a dedicated **client trust account (IOLTA)**, never the operating account.
- **No commingling:** firm money never sits in trust (except a small allowed cushion for bank fees where your state permits).
- **No borrowing across clients:** you may **never** use one client's funds to cover another's — every client's ledger must stay **≥ $0** at all times.
- **Earn before you move:** unearned fees/advances stay in trust; move fees to operating **only as earned**, and advanced costs only per policy.
- **Prompt disbursement:** pay out promptly once funds clear; don't let balances linger.
- **Disputed funds stay put:** any amount in dispute (e.g., a contested lien) **remains in trust** until resolved.

## Per-matter ledger

Maintain an individual ledger for **each client/matter** showing every deposit and disbursement and a running balance. The disbursement statement the client signs ([27](27-settlement-and-disbursement-workflow.md)) must match this ledger exactly.

## Three-way reconciliation (do this monthly)

The heart of trust accounting. All three must be **equal**:

1. **Bank balance** — the reconciled trust-account bank statement balance.
2. **Book balance** — the firm's trust checkbook/ledger balance.
3. **Sum of client ledgers** — the total of every individual matter's balance.

```
Adjusted bank balance  =  Book balance  =  Σ (all client ledger balances)
```

If they don't tie, **stop and find the difference before any further disbursement.** Document the reconciliation, and have an **attorney review and sign off** each month.

## Controls

- Authorized **attorney signatory** on trust checks; no signature stamps left unsecured.
- No ATM/debit cards or cash withdrawals on the trust account.
- Dual review on disbursements above a firm-set threshold.
- Deposit settlement checks and confirm they've **cleared** before disbursing against them.
- Scan every trust check and receipt into the matter file.

## Retention

Keep complete trust records — ledgers, statements, reconciliations, canceled checks — for your state's required period (commonly **five years or more** after the matter ends under Rule 1.15). See [close-out & retention](40-file-closeout-reconciliation-and-retention.md).

## Red flags (investigate immediately)

- Any client ledger going negative.
- Bank balance below the sum of client ledgers ("shortfall").
- Round-dollar transfers to operating not tied to an earned fee.
- Old, un-disbursed balances.

## Attorney gate

Trust withdrawals, fee transfers, disputed-fund decisions, and the monthly reconciliation sign-off are **licensed-attorney responsibilities**. When in doubt, leave the money in trust and call your state bar's ethics hotline.
