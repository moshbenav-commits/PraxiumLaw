# Certificate of Records (COR)

**Category:** Medical Record Tracking · Litigation readiness  
**Source:** Lit Audit SOP  
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).

---

## What it is

A **Certificate of Records (COR)** certifies that the provider’s bills and records for a period are **complete and accurate**.

## When required

- Client is **done treating**, or  
- Case is **transferring to litigation**

Request bills, records, **and COR** for every provider. Each billing period/batch should have its own COR.

## Audit rule

- Missing COR = **not verified**  
- Report which providers **lack** COR (not a laundry list of who has them)

## Why it matters

Complete records + COR up front avoids **supplements** later. Training emphasis: do it right the first time.

## Product rule

Mark providers `COR: received | missing | N/A (still treating)`. Block “demand complete” or “lit transfer ready” flags when required CORs are missing (attorney override allowed).
