# Non-Medical Liens & Claims on the Recovery

**Category:** Settlement & Disbursement / Compliance
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md)

---

## Purpose

Medical and subrogation liens ([29](29-health-insurance-subrogation.md)) aren't the only claims on a settlement. Missing a **non-medical** lien can mean paying twice, disciplinary exposure, or handing the client money that legally isn't theirs. Identify these **early** and confirm them **before disbursement** ([25](25-disbursement-sheet-preparation.md)/[27](27-settlement-and-disbursement-workflow.md)); when in doubt, **hold the disputed amount in trust** ([45](45-trust-accounting-and-reconciliation.md)).

## Types to screen for

| Claim | What to watch |
|-------|---------------|
| **Child-support arrears** | A support agency may have a lien/levy on the recovery; confirm the payoff before releasing funds. Reducing your fee is a last resort. |
| **Bankruptcy** | A personal-injury claim can be **property of the bankruptcy estate**. The client must disclose it; a trustee may control the proceeds (Ch. 7) or a plan may apply (Ch. 13). **Do not disburse** around a bankruptcy — coordinate with the trustee/bankruptcy counsel. |
| **Probate / deceased client** | If the client dies, the claim/recovery flows through the **estate**; confirm the proper personal representative and authority before paying. |
| **Divorce / family law** | A pending divorce may create a **marital interest** or court order affecting the funds. |
| **Government liens** | Tax liens (IRS/state), restitution, or public-benefit overpayments can attach. |
| **Prior attorney lien** | A previously discharged firm may assert a [charging lien / quantum meruit](28-client-termination-and-attorney-liens.md). |

## Workflow

1. **Screen at intake and again pre-settlement** — ask about child support, bankruptcy (current or recent), divorce, tax issues, prior counsel, and (if the client died) the estate.
2. **Verify** any suspected lien in writing; get the current payoff.
3. **Hold** disputed/unresolved amounts in trust; **do not disburse** until cleared.
4. **Reflect** confirmed liens on the [disbursement sheet](25-disbursement-sheet-preparation.md) and pay per priority.

## Attorney gate

Whether a claim attaches, its priority, and how to resolve it are **legal determinations** for the attorney (often with bankruptcy/family/probate counsel). Never disburse over an unresolved non-medical lien.
