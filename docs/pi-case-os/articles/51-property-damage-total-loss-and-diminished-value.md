# Property Damage: Total Loss, Diminished Value, Rental & Deductible

## Purpose

Deeper property-damage handling beyond the timing rules in [PD & liability timing](03-property-damage-liability-timing.md). PD is often the client's most urgent, tangible problem — handle it well and early; it builds trust and **must be resolved before any bodily-injury release** ([settlement, art. 27](27-settlement-and-disbursement-workflow.md)).

## Repair vs. total loss

- **Total loss** when repair cost (plus salvage) meets the carrier's threshold of the vehicle's value.
- On a total loss, the measure is **actual cash value (ACV)** — pre-loss fair market value. Verify the carrier's valuation against comparable local vehicles; challenge low ACVs with comps, options, condition, and recent work.
- Watch **salvage/title** handling and any **GAP** coverage if the client owes more than ACV.

## Diminished value (state-specific)

- Even after quality repairs, a vehicle can be worth less simply because it was wrecked — **inherent diminished value**.
- Availability and proof requirements **vary by state and by first- vs third-party**; where viable, support it with a DV appraisal. Confirm your state.

## Rental / loss of use

- The client is generally owed **transportation during repair or while a total loss is resolved** — rental (via the at-fault carrier or the client's rental coverage) or **loss-of-use** value if they didn't rent.
- Don't let the carrier drag repair timelines and then cut off rental.

## Deductible recovery

- If the client used their **own collision** coverage to fix the car, their carrier's **subrogation** should recover the deductible back for the client — track it and confirm it's returned.

## Coordination

- Resolve PD before signing the 3P **release** (a broad release can waive PD, loss of use, and the deductible) — see [settlement](27-settlement-and-disbursement-workflow.md).
- Log PD costs/recoveries so nothing is missed at [disbursement](25-disbursement-sheet-preparation.md).

## Attorney gate

Whether to open the client's first-party coverage, release language, and any DV/loss-of-use dispute strategy are **attorney decisions**. Staff coordinate valuation, rental, and deductible recovery.
