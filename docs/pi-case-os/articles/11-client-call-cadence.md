# Client Call Cadence: 3-Day, 7-Day, and Monthly

**Category:** Case Manager Tips  
**Source:** Personal Injury Workflow; Case Manage / Cleaner Fish; 7-day Needs List task  
**Audience:** Case managers

---

Clients fire firms that go silent. Training builds a simple cadence.

## Three days after intake / DOL tasks

Call to confirm:

- Needs List homework (declarations, photos, estimates, bills)
- Attendance at each referred provider
- Any mail bills (ambulance, ER physician, radiology)

## Three to seven days

Broader Needs List check-in if anything remains outstanding. Training also references a **seven-day** call pattern alongside the three-day task.

## Every two weeks (alternating)

1. **Client week:** How is treatment? Pain? Transportation barriers? Still attending?
2. **Provider week:** Balances, last treatment date (LTD), next treatment date (NTD), no-shows, confirm a future appointment exists

Receptionist turnover can leave a client without a next appointment. Provider calls catch that.

## Thirty-day case review

Formal review of MRI / pain management / outstanding issues (see related article).

## Property damage exception

If rental or repairs are pending, cadence becomes **daily** pressure on the adjuster — not the biweekly treatment rhythm.

## What “good” sounds like

Short, specific, documented. Training emphasizes notes with dates so that if a client claims “I called fifteen times,” the file shows the actual contact history.
