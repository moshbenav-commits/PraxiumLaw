# PraxiumLaw Knowledge Articles

Recommended, **white-label** standard-operating procedures for personal-injury and related practice, organized by department. Written for **any firm** and aimed especially at **smaller/newer firms** building their operations.

## Disclosure (applies to every article)

These articles are **recommended procedures and operational education, not legal advice, and not your firm's rules.** Before using any script, checklist, or process in a live matter:

1. **Cross-reference** each procedure against your firm's own policies and your jurisdiction's law and ethics rules.
2. Have a **licensed attorney** review and approve.
3. Edit all related documents (letters, forms, fee agreements) — do not use templates as-is.
