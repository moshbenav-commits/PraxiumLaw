# Treatment Gaps and MRI Timing

**Category:** Treatment Milestone Checklist  
**Source:** Personal Injury Workflow; 5-Week and 30-Day Case Review training  
**Audience:** Case managers

---

## Gaps kill value

Training is explicit: if there are **gaps in treatment**, the insurance company will argue the client was not hurt. Book initial appointments **the same day as intake** when possible — pain management or clinic **and** chiropractic or physical therapy.

Clients sometimes attend only one provider because they are used to a single doctor for everything. Case managers must confirm attendance at **each** referred provider, not only the first appointment on the calendar.

## When to push for MRI

- If there is **no improvement in about one month**, the client needs an MRI pathway through the treating doctor.
- At about **one to two months** without improvement, MRI should be in motion.
- If it has been about **eight weeks** without an MRI, call the pain doctor and find out who dropped the ball — client, office, or provider.
- Training states that missing MRI **reduces settlement value**.

## What to read on imaging

On the 30-day / 5-week review:

- Confirm you are reading an **MRI**, not an X-ray
- Capture interpretation language (for example disc bulge levels and measurements)
- Attach the MRI to the case review fields
- Read pain-management notes for pain scores and **plans** (injections, surgery)

Routine chiro notes are lower priority than pain management, neurology EMGs, and MRIs.

## Treatment path (as practiced)

ER / acute care → conservative care (chiro / PT) → pain management → MRI → injections → surgery only if conservative care and injections fail. Training warns against sending someone to surgery immediately to “hit” a large policy limit.
