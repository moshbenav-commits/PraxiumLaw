## Purpose

Premises cases (slip/trip-and-fall, negligent security, unsafe conditions) run the same treatment/demand/settlement spine as auto PI, but **liability is harder and evidence disappears fast**. This article covers what's different; use the [lifecycle map](00-case-lifecycle-and-workflow-map.md) for the shared phases.

## What's different from auto PI

- **No police report or adjuster hand-off on day one.** You must build liability yourself.
- **Duty depends on the visitor's status** (invitee/licensee/trespasser) and each state's premises-liability rules — verify your jurisdiction.
- **Notice is the battleground:** did the owner create the hazard, or know (or should have known) about it and fail to fix it? Recurring-condition and "mode of operation" theories vary by state.
- **Comparative fault is common** (open-and-obvious conditions, footwear, distraction) and can reduce or bar recovery.

## Evidence — act immediately (it vanishes)

- **Preservation/spoliation letter** to the property owner/manager for surveillance video, incident reports, and maintenance/inspection logs — send fast; video is often overwritten in days.
- Photograph the exact condition, lighting, and surroundings; measure where relevant.
- Identify the property owner, tenant, and any maintenance contractor (multiple potential defendants and policies).
- Locate witnesses and any incident report the client signed.

## Coverage & claims

- Look for **commercial general liability (CGL)** and any med-pay; there is no UM/UIM analog — the recovery source is the premises owner's/operator's liability policy.
- Open the claim with the correct carrier once identified; letters of representation as in [opening claims](04-opening-3p-and-1p-claims.md).

## From there

Treatment management, records, demand, negotiation, settlement, and disbursement follow the standard articles. Liability development and any expert (e.g., safety/engineering) are **attorney-directed**.

## Attorney gate

Liability theory, defendant selection, expert retention, and settlement authority are **attorney decisions**.
