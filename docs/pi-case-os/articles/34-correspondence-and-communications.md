# Correspondence & Communications Standards

**Category:** Operations
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md)

---

## Purpose

Consistent, documented communication with clients, carriers, providers, and courts protects the file, the client relationship, and the firm. Every meaningful communication should end up **in the case file**.

## Core rules

- **Everything to the file:** forward and save emails, letters, texts, and call notes to the matter; a communication that isn't in the file didn't happen for purposes of the record.
- **Route through the office:** ask carriers, providers, and opposing parties to direct communications to the firm's fax/email of record so nothing lands in a personal inbox.
- **Certified when it matters:** anything with legal effect or a deadline (liens, drop letters, preservation/spoliation, statutory notices) goes certified, return receipt requested — and the receipt is saved.
- **Confidentiality & privilege:** don't include privileged strategy or harmful notes in documents that third parties may see; follow the [redaction checklist](19-redaction-checklist.md).

## Channels

- **Client:** confirm preferred language and channel; keep a call cadence ([`11-client-call-cadence.md`](11-client-call-cadence.md)); log texts and calls.
- **Carriers/providers:** use letters of representation, records/bills requests, and status follow-ups from your template set; calendar every follow-up.
- **Courts:** follow the court's e-filing/service rules; save stamped copies to the file.

## Naming & saving (adapt to your system)

Adopt one consistent convention so anyone can find a document, e.g. `Party – Type – Date – Initials` (`INS-3P – LOR – 2026-07-10 – RS`). Save to the correct section of the [document taxonomy](18-document-taxonomy.md), leave an activity note, and attach the file.

## Attorney gate

Substantive legal correspondence (demands, releases, pleadings, settlement communications) is **attorney-reviewed** before it goes out.
