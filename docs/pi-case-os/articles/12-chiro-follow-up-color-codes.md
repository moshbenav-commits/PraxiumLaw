# Chiro and Clinic Follow-Up Color Codes

**Category:** Doctor Network Coordination  
**Source:** Med training notes  
**Audience:** Junior callers, medical management staff

---

Treatment tracking in training uses shared sheets (in addition to the case system) with **color codes** so callers can sort active work instantly.

## Nevada chiro sheet colors

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Red | Completed |
| White | Active |
| Purple | No show |
| Yellow | Close to stopping treatment |
| Green | Treatment on hold |
| Highlighted blue | Not confirmed |

## What to update

- Treatment dates
- Claimants and policy limits (when known)
- Estimated balances
- LTD / NTD (last treatment date / next treatment date)
- Notes for non-responsive clinics

## Lists to generate

- No-shows
- Close to stop treatment
- Stop-ats
- Limits
- Non-responsive providers

## NPIM-specific notes

- Yellow: estimated balance getting close to **Stop At**
- Purple: no-shows (NS)
- **PRN:** person reporting no pain
- New clients: confirm initial appointment and follow-ups; call after appointment and after referral

## Cadence

Training references **3-day chiro** tasks in the case system and ongoing sheet updates. Always keep calling until necessary information is obtained — “non-responsive” is a status to work, not a closing code.
