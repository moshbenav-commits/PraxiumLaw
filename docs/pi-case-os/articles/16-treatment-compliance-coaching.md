# Treatment Compliance Coaching: Getting Clients to Do What the Case Needs

**Category:** Pain Education & Treatment Compliance  
**Source:** Personal Injury Workflow; Case Audit; Treatment Tracker; med training notes  
**Audience:** Case managers  
**Sensitivity:** Coaching supports medical recommendations — it does not replace physician judgment

---

Personal injury cases fail when clients do not treat. The job of compliance coaching is simple: **get the client to do what the case needs** — show up, follow care, stay consistent, and avoid gaps insurers will use against them.

## What the case needs from the client

1. **Start care immediately** — chiro or PT **and** pain management or a general physician (both tracks).
2. **Attend every referred provider** — not only the first appointment on the list.
3. **Accept recommended treatment** when the doctor offers it (training notes: say yes to offered care).
4. **Get imaging when conservative care stalls** — MRI pathway by about one month without improvement; escalate by about eight weeks.
5. **Understand injections and surgery** — declining care can mean a lower settlement (often tied to medical specials) and future bills on the client’s own insurance.
6. **Report pain consistently** — swinging from 9–10 to 1 without explanation looks bad on records.
7. **Keep monthly visits while the demand is out** — so there is no gap during record gathering and adjuster review.
8. **Stay off contradictory social media** — investigators and spoliation risk.

## When to coach

| Moment | Message |
|--------|---------|
| Intake | Book appointments today; explain gaps kill claims |
| 3-day / 7-day call | Did you go? Both providers? Re-book no-shows |
| Monthly / biweekly | How is treatment? Provider confirms attendance and next date |
| MRI due | Ladder: massage/chiro alone is not enough if structural injury |
| After MRI | Injections vs “wait and hope”; attorney if client wants to stop |
| Demand window | Monthly evaluations until an offer — not “disappear for four months” |
