# Document Taxonomy: How to Classify Everything That Comes In

**Category:** Records & Operations  
**Source:** Training PI — Documents Saving Overview  
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).

---

Every fax, mail, email, text, photo, or voicemail should land in a **type**. Type drives where it is filed and what tabs/tasks update.

## Types

| Type | Examples | Action |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Intake** | Signed packet, TAR/police report, accident photos, ID, insurance cards, defendant info | Update intake, meds, treatment, auto & health insurance tabs |
| **Medical** | Records, bills, futures estimates, COR | Meds tab; lien balances; demand readiness |
| **Auto insurance** | PD, BI, MedPay, coverage, demand, settlement correspondence | Insurance-auto / negotiation / PD tabs |
| **Health insurance / subrogation** | Health plan liens, subro notices | Insurance-health / subro workflow |
| **Invoice / receipt** | Police report fees, limits pulls, copy fees, medical records fees | Expenses tab (feeds disbursement) |
| **Pleadings** | Complaint, AOS, answers, motions, scheduling orders | Pleadings / deadlines (lit) |
| **Miscellaneous** | Voicemail, leads, reviews | Route to lead or activity notes |

## Channels

Training materials treat **fax lines** (records / filing / info) and physical mail as primary inbound sources. Product should support fax, mail scan, email, client text, and portal upload with the same taxonomy.

## Why it matters

Misfiled items cause wrong Meds totals, missed expenses at disbursement, and demand delays. Classification is not clerical busywork — it is case integrity.
