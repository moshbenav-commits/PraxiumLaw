# Wage-Loss & Lost-Earning-Capacity Documentation

**Category:** Damages
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).

## Purpose

Lost income is a core damage that is routinely under-documented and therefore under-paid. This SOP defines what to collect so wage loss survives adjuster scrutiny and supports the [demand](39-demand-letter-drafting.md). Two different claims: **past lost wages** (already missed) and **lost earning capacity** (future/diminished ability to earn).

## Tie it to the injury

Lost time must be **medically supported** — get the treating provider's **off-work/disability notes** (dates, restrictions) connecting the inability to work to the crash injuries. Wage loss without a medical basis gets denied.

## Past lost wages — documentation

**W-2 employees:**
- [ ] **Employer wage-loss verification letter** (dates missed, rate, hours, position)
- [ ] Recent **pay stubs** (pre-injury baseline)
- [ ] **W-2s** / prior-year earnings
- [ ] Record of **PTO/sick leave** used (a compensable loss even if "paid")

**Self-employed / 1099:**
- [ ] **Tax returns** (1–3 years) and **1099s**
- [ ] **Profit & loss** statements, invoices, contracts lost/cancelled
- [ ] Bookkeeping records showing the downturn tied to the injury period

## Lost earning capacity (future)

- More complex than past wages — reflects a **reduced ability to earn** going forward.
- Often requires **vocational** and **economic experts** and the medical picture of permanent restrictions/impairment.
- Flag early; expert work needs lead time and attorney authorization.

## Collection workflow

1. At intake, capture employment, role, rate, and whether they missed work ([intake needs list](01-intake-needs-list.md)).
2. Send the **wage-loss verification** request to the employer; calendar follow-up ([records retrieval](52-records-retrieval-and-vendors.md)).
3. Gather the off-work notes alongside medical records ([medical LOR](21-medical-lor-workflow.md)).
4. Reconcile the numbers and add the wage-loss summary to the [demand](39-demand-letter-drafting.md).

## Attorney gate

Valuation of lost earning capacity and retention of vocational/economic experts are **attorney decisions**. Staff collect and organize the documentation.
