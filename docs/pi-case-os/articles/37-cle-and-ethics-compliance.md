## Purpose

Keep attorneys licensed and the firm's ethics obligations met. This is a compliance backstop, not legal advice; each attorney is responsible for their own bar's requirements.

## Continuing legal education (CLE)

- Track each attorney's **CLE cycle, credit total, and specialty credits** (ethics, technology, elimination-of-bias) required by their state bar.
- Calendar deadlines with a buffer; keep certificates of completion in a compliance folder.
- Note that CLE rules differ by state and by admission date — verify each attorney's bar.

## Recurring ethics touchpoints for a PI practice

- **Fees (Rule 1.5):** contingency agreements in writing; fees reasonable and disclosed; any fee-sharing/referral arrangement compliant and client-disclosed.
- **Trust accounting (Rule 1.15):** client funds segregated; ledgered and reconciled; records retained (commonly ≥5 years); no commingling. See [`27-settlement-and-disbursement-workflow.md`](27-settlement-and-disbursement-workflow.md).
- **Withdrawal (Rule 1.16):** proper notice, file return, refund of unearned fees. See [`28-client-termination-and-attorney-liens.md`](28-client-termination-and-attorney-liens.md).
- **Advertising & solicitation:** intake/lead practices follow state advertising rules. See [`35-lead-intake-and-signup-operations.md`](35-lead-intake-and-signup-operations.md).
- **Confidentiality & conflicts:** conflict checks at intake ([`13-conflict-check-and-related-cases.md`](13-conflict-check-and-related-cases.md)); privilege protected in correspondence and redaction.
- **Supervision:** non-lawyer staff work under attorney supervision; the attorney gates in these articles reflect that.

## Attorney gate

Bar compliance and ethics determinations are the **attorney's/firm's responsibility**. Use your state's rules of professional conduct — the model rules cited here are only a starting point.
