# Demand Letter Drafting

**Category:** Demand
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md) — letters of protection; statutes of limitations

---

## Purpose

Turn a complete file into a persuasive demand that maximizes the client's recovery. This is the drafting step; the pre-draft reconciliation lives in the [demand prep checklist](09-demand-prep-checklist.md). **Do not draft until the demand package is complete** — a demand built on a half-reconciled file undersells the case.

## Anatomy of the demand

1. **Introduction & representation** — client, date of loss, that the firm represents the client, and the demand's purpose.
2. **Facts / liability** — clear narrative of how the incident happened and why the insured is at fault; cite the police report and physical evidence. Keep it factual.
3. **Injuries & treatment** — the injury story chronologically: complaints, diagnostics (imaging), treatment course, procedures/injections/surgery, and current status. Tie every claim to a record.
4. **Medical specials** — an itemized bill summary by provider with totals; note any letters of protection and future-care estimates (supported, not invented).
5. **Other damages** — wage loss (with documentation), out-of-pocket, and, where appropriate, pain-and-suffering framing consistent with your jurisdiction.
6. **Demand & deadline** — the settlement figure and a response deadline; calendar it.

## Drafting discipline

- Every asserted fact must be **supported by a document in the file**; don't reference records you don't have.
- Attach exhibits in a consistent order (records, bills, imaging, report, wage docs); **redact** per the [redaction checklist](19-redaction-checklist.md) before anything leaves the office.
- Send a companion **UM/UIM demand** to the client's own carrier when first-party coverage is in play.
- Keep tone professional; overstatement invites a lowball.

## Timing & limits watch

- Confirm the **statute of limitations** is not at risk before relying on negotiation; if it's close, coordinate with [litigation handoff](31-litigation-handoff-and-management.md).
- Match the demand strategy to available coverage/limits ([policy limits](15-policy-limits-and-claimants.md), [coverage reference](33-auto-insurance-coverage-reference.md)).

## Attorney gate

**The attorney reviews and approves the demand before it is sent.** Settlement figures and any policy-limits demand strategy are attorney decisions. After send, calendar the response and keep the client on periodic evaluations while the demand is outstanding.
