## What this recording is

A live sit-down intake: declarations page, health insurance, prior claims, property damage, treatment plan, fee agreement walkthrough, and client coaching.

## Coaching lines worth productizing

### Health insurance at urgent care

When the client needs care quickly (e.g. leaving town):

- Direct to **quick care / urgent care**
- **Yes, use your health insurance** — “That’s the best way”
- Collect photo of health insurance card

### Prior claims questions (ask every time)

- “Have you ever had any prior accidents?”
- “Nobody ever made a claim or opened a claim?”
- “Do you have any other claims — slip and fall, work comp, anything?”

### Property damage vs injuries (client script)

> You can speak to the adjuster about the **property damage**. It’s just not about the accident or the injuries.

> Next time call us **before** you talk to them… They’re not your friends.

> You can talk to them about your car — “I need my car fixed, I need a rental” — **nothing about injuries**.

Why: adjusters lock in incomplete injury lists (“you only told me about your neck”) and use that later.

### Liability timing

- PD may take **two weeks to 30 days** unless the other side admits fault
- “Just because someone hits you doesn’t mean they automatically fix your car”
- If client needs a car **fast**, open **1P** (firm helps) so client is not talking to carriers alone

### Treatment / records

- Teach clients “everything to do in an accident” for lifetime referrals
- Gaps: “If you have any significant gaps then they’re gonna say that you’re not injured”
- Tell doctors it is for an **accident** so it is in the records

### Fee agreement (plain language)

- Contingency: **1/3** if recovered without court; **40%** if court needed; **no fee** if no recovery
- Firm advances costs (police report, policy limits, postage, copies)
- Client can fire with written notice; time billed to new counsel
- No loans (state bar)
- Never settle without client authority
- Disbursement spreadsheet: client / firm / doctors
- Do not file bankruptcy on medical bills
