# Reduction Requests & Provider Negotiation

**Category:** Settlement & Disbursement
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md)

---

## Purpose

When the settlement will not cover all medical balances in full, the firm asks providers and lienholders to voluntarily reduce their balances so the client nets a fair recovery. A reduction request is an **offer to compromise** — it should say so.

## Before you send

- Pull the reduction percentages from the reviewed [disbursement sheet](25-disbursement-sheet-preparation.md); only send the requests you were tasked to send.
- Send to the **entity that actually holds the balance** — often a lien-purchaser or collection assignee, not the original provider.
- **Do not notify providers who should not be notified.** As a general practice many firms do not send reduction requests to hospitals, ER physicians, ER radiology, or ambulance companies that have no notice of the representation — confirm your firm's policy and ethics rules first.

## Types of request (general framework)

| Type | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Short** | Standard ask; client name, DOL, DOB, balance, and the compromise language. |
| **Long** | Adds the settlement figure and a proposed disbursement breakdown; used when a provider wants to see the whole picture or when asking for a large cut. |
| **Hospital** | Some states require statutory hospital discounts for qualifying uninsured patients — cite the applicable statute for **your** state; do not assume another state's percentage applies. |
| **Write-off / large (>50%)** | For cases that under-performed; asks the provider to write off to avoid an interpleader. |

## Working the requests

- Keep the task open and calendar a follow-up (e.g., one week). Do not assume "sent" means "received."
- If nothing comes back, call on a set cadence (e.g., every other day) and escalate to a supervising attorney if it stalls past your firm's threshold.
- Consider a **partial disbursement** when one slow lienholder is holding up everyone else.
- Log each approved reduction on the disbursement sheet and alert the attorney once all are in.

## Counters

Providers may counter. Negotiate toward the client's net while documenting every offer/counter in the file. Final acceptance of any reduction is an **attorney decision**.

## Attorney gate

Reduction percentages and any fee reduction are attorney-approved. Reduction and disbursement letters should be attorney-reviewed before send.
