# File Close-Out, Reconciliation & Retention

**Category:** Settlement & Disbursement / Operations
**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.
**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).
**References:** [`../REFERENCES.md`](../REFERENCES.md) — Model Rule 1.15 (trust records & retention)

---

## Purpose

Properly close a matter after disbursement so nothing is left owing, the trust account balances, the client is taken care of, and the file is retained per your ethics rules. A case isn't "done" when the client's check is handed over — it's done when everything reconciles.

## Reconcile the money

- Confirm **every** provider/lienholder check has cleared or a receipt is back; the [client trust account](27-settlement-and-disbursement-workflow.md) must balance to zero for the matter.
- Verify all liens are satisfied and documented — private, [health/Medicare/Medicaid/ERISA subrogation](29-health-insurance-subrogation.md), workers'-comp, child-support, and any [funding loan](25-disbursement-sheet-preparation.md).
- Confirm the firm's earned fee and costs are moved out of trust and recorded; the client's net matches the signed disbursement statement.

## Close the file

- Upload the fully signed disbursement/settlement statement, release, and any government-benefit forms.
- Send the client a closing letter: what was resolved, the final numbers, and that the matter is closed.
- Return client property/original documents the client is entitled to.
- Complete case KPIs / metrics and move the matter to **Closed** in the [case-management system](36-case-management-system-setup.md).

## Retain per ethics rules

- Keep **client-trust and financial records** for your jurisdiction's required period (commonly **five years or more** after the representation ends under Model Rule 1.15 — verify your state).
- Apply your firm's [retention & destruction schedule](38-firm-operations-and-office-policies.md) to the rest of the file; don't destroy anything early, and destroy securely when the period ends.
- Preserve confidentiality of retained PII and medical records.

## Loose-end check before closing

- [ ] Trust balanced to zero for the matter
- [ ] All liens satisfied and proof filed
- [ ] Property damage fully resolved
- [ ] Client paid and closing letter sent
- [ ] Signed disbursement statement + release on file
- [ ] File retained per schedule

## Attorney gate

Trust reconciliation and final disbursement approval remain under **licensed-attorney control**. Do not close a matter with an unbalanced trust ledger or an unresolved lien.
