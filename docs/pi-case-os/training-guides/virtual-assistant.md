# Training Guide — Virtual Assistant (Remote)

**PraxiumLaw role:** `staff` (often limited permissions)  
**Reports to:** Case manager · Paralegal · Office manager

---

## Purpose

Remote support for **repeatable, checklist-driven tasks** — data entry, document filing, appointment confirmation, records follow-up, and inbox hygiene — without client legal advice or adjuster negotiations on injuries.

Training corpus includes VA-specific SOPs (sourcing, English requirements, task boundaries).

---

## What you own (typical)

| Task | Boundary |
|------|----------|
| Data entry from intake forms | Double-check legibility with case manager |
| Document rename + upload | Taxonomy required |
| Records follow-up calls | “Checking status of records request” — no medical advice |
| Appointment reminders | Confirm dates; log in tasks |
| CRM hygiene | Contacts, adjuster phone/email updates |
| Template prep | Fill non-legal fields only — attorney-reviewed templates |
| Lead inbox triage | Flag hot leads — attorney/case manager converts |

---

## What you do NOT do

- Sign clients  
- Discuss liability, injuries, or settlement with carriers  
- Send demand or reduction letters without attorney-approved send  
- Redact medical records for carrier without checklist training  

---

## Communication standards

- Professional English (firm standard)  
- Use firm-approved scripts only  
- Log all client/adjuster contacts in matter Notes or tasks  
- Escalate angry clients, media, or legal questions immediately
