# Role Certification Path

**Category:** Training & operations

**Recommended procedure** — cross-reference against your firm's policy and jurisdiction before use.

**White-label:** Any firm. See [`../DISCLOSURE.md`](../DISCLOSURE.md).

---

## How certification works

Staff advance through **three levels**:

| Level | Earned by |
|-------|-----------|
| **Trainee** | Completed required reading + supervised 1st task + knowledge check sign-off by supervisor |
| **Certified** | Demonstrated 5+ core competencies (hands-on demonstration) + supervisor sign-off + passing knowledge assessment |
| **Senior / Lead** | 6+ months in role + led training of newer staff + handled complex edge cases + attorney endorsement |

**Attorney gates always apply**: Demand send, settlement authority, reduction percentages, client strategy, drops, and litigations require attorney approval regardless of certification level.

## Intake Specialist

**Purpose:** Qualify leads, collect intake documents, run conflict checks, execute signature packets, and hand off a complete file.

**Required reading**

- [23 — Intake forms & signature packet](../articles/23-intake-forms-and-signature-packet.md)
- [01 — Intake needs list](../articles/01-intake-needs-list.md)
- [13 — Conflict check & related cases](../articles/13-conflict-check-and-related-cases.md)
- [04 — Opening 3P & 1P claims](../articles/04-opening-3p-and-1p-claims.md)
- [47 — Inbound lead intake call script](../articles/47-inbound-lead-intake-call-script.md)
- [18 — Document taxonomy](../articles/18-document-taxonomy.md)

**Competencies checklist**

- [ ] Schedule and run intake appointment using firm-approved script
- [ ] Collect and verify client declarations, ID, and health insurance
- [ ] Execute signature packet (fee agreement, POA, HIPAA, disclosure) — no omissions
- [ ] Run conflict check (defendant + owner if different) and escalate hits to attorney
- [ ] Generate and print correct intake forms (English/Spanish per firm)
- [ ] Open 3P claim on day one; coach client on PD-only discussion
- [ ] Walk Needs List with client and populate Matter Intake tab
- [ ] Scan and classify all intake documents with correct taxonomy
- [ ] Book initial medical appointments (two tracks: chiro + GP/pain mgmt)

**Assessment:** Supervised end-to-end intake (attorney or case manager sign-off) + written knowledge check (conflict scenarios, Needs List top 10, signature packet completeness).

**Level criteria**

| Level | Standard |
|-------|----------|
| **Trainee** | 1 supervised intake, passes knowledge check |
| **Certified** | 10+ unsupervised intakes, 0 critical errors (omitted signature, wrong conflict check) |
| **Senior** | 50+ intakes, coaches new intake staff, handles edge cases (minors, out-of-state, Medicare) |

## Case Manager

**Purpose:** Drive files from intake through demand, negotiation, and settlement prep. Coach clients, maintain Meds ledger, chase records, and prepare attorney-ready packages.

**Required reading**

- [08 — Meds tab & record hygiene](../articles/08-meds-tab-and-record-hygiene.md)
- [21 — Medical LOR workflow](../articles/21-medical-lor-workflow.md)
- [20 — Certificate of records](../articles/20-certificate-of-records.md)
- [09 — Demand prep checklist](../articles/09-demand-prep-checklist.md)
- [10 — Settlement calculator scenarios](../articles/10-settlement-calculator-scenarios.md)
- [07 — Thirty-day case review](../articles/07-thirty-day-case-review.md)
- [11 — Client call cadence](../articles/11-client-call-cadence.md)
- [16 — Treatment compliance coaching](../articles/16-treatment-compliance-coaching.md)

**Competencies checklist**

- [ ] Maintain Meds ledger daily (provider, balance, lien holder, futures estimate, COR status)
- [ ] Run Needs List verification at handoff from intake
- [ ] Request medical records via LOR; follow up on non-responses
- [ ] Identify treatment gaps and coach client on compliance
- [ ] Conduct 30-day case review (MRI plan, outlier treatment, limits check)
- [ ] Assemble demand preparation worksheet (exhibits, chronology, medical summaries)
- [ ] Calculate settlement scenarios from Meds data (submit to attorney for reductions)
- [ ] Track 3P/1P insurance acknowledgments and liability acceptance
- [ ] Flag alerts to attorney: no-show, no MRI by ~8 weeks, treatment gap, client stop request

**Assessment:** Shadow and own 5 active cases simultaneously; case manager + attorney spot-check Meds maintenance and demand assembly.

**Level criteria**

| Level | Standard |
|-------|----------|
| **Trainee** | 3–5 active cases with daily supervision; 1 complete 30-day review observed |
| **Certified** | 8–12 active cases, daily Meds maintenance, 2+ demands assembled without attorney rework |
| **Senior** | 15+ active cases, mentors intake/VAs, conducts quarterly case audits, identifies process gaps |

## Paralegal

**Purpose:** Draft and assemble litigation-ready documents, support demand and settlement packages, maintain pleadings/filings hygiene, and run firm workflows under attorney supervision.

**Required reading**

- [24 — Medical documents reference](../articles/24-medical-documents-reference.md)
- [18 — Document taxonomy](../articles/18-document-taxonomy.md)
- [19 — Redaction checklist](../articles/19-redaction-checklist.md)
- [21 — Medical LOR workflow](../articles/21-medical-lor-workflow.md)
- [39 — Demand letter drafting](../articles/39-demand-letter-drafting.md)
- [31 — Litigation handoff & management](../articles/31-litigation-handoff-and-management.md)
- [26 — Reduction requests & negotiation](../articles/26-reduction-requests-and-negotiation.md)

**Competencies checklist**

- [ ] Redact medical records per checklist before carrier send (0 omissions)
- [ ] Draft medical LOR per template; merge HIPAA and specialty codes
- [ ] Generate and merge mail templates (acknowledgment, COR follow-up, lien notices)
- [ ] Assemble demand exhibit binders (medical summaries, bills, prior records, COR)
- [ ] Draft demand letter using demand worksheet order and attorney guidance
- [ ] Draft reduction request letters (short / long templates) — attorney sets terms
- [ ] Generate transfer-to-litigation letters; maintain filing index
- [ ] Classify and tag documents per taxonomy (MR, B, FE, COR, Intake, etc.)
- [ ] Run conflict check workflows and initiate task assignments

**Assessment:** Paralegal + attorney review of redacted packets, drafted letters, and demand packages. Spot-check for compliance (HIPAA, taxonomy accuracy, no unintended disclosures).

**Level criteria**

| Level | Standard |
|-------|----------|
| **Trainee** | 1 supervised demand draft, 3 redacted packets reviewed, passes redaction knowledge check |
| **Certified** | 5+ demand drafts with <2 attorney revisions each, zero redaction errors on 20+ packets |
| **Senior** | 20+ demand drafts, trains staff on redaction/taxonomy, handles complex multi-provider cases |

## Billing / Disbursements

**Purpose:** Manage financial hygiene on closed and closing files: trust accounting support, fee calculation, provider payments, expense reimbursement, and disbursement checklists under attorney approval.

**Required reading**

- [25 — Disbursement sheet preparation](../articles/25-disbursement-sheet-preparation.md)
- [10 — Settlement calculator scenarios](../articles/10-settlement-calculator-scenarios.md)
- [27 — Settlement & disbursement workflow](../articles/27-settlement-and-disbursement-workflow.md)
- [45 — Trust accounting & reconciliation](../articles/45-trust-accounting-and-reconciliation.md)
- [30 — Minors compromise & court approval](../articles/30-minors-compromise-and-court-approval.md)
- [29 — Health insurance subrogation](../articles/29-health-insurance-subrogation.md)

**Competencies checklist**

- [ ] Extract provider balances from Meds ledger and verify totals against settlement scenarios
- [ ] Calculate contingency fee per firm policy and fee agreement (confirm attorney reductions first)
- [ ] Itemize expenses (police report fees, records retrieval, copy costs) for reimbursement
- [ ] Prepare disbursement checklist (all providers scheduled, fees calculated, expenses itemized)
- [ ] Flag Medicare/Medicaid repayment obligations to attorney + case manager
- [ ] Reconcile lien amounts per provider agreements and settlement reductions
- [ ] Support minor's compromise logistics (court approval, structured settlement setup)
- [ ] Coordinate trust / IOLTA transfer per firm accounting SOP (if outside PraxiumLaw)
- [ ] Maintain closure documentation for audit trail

**Assessment:** Supervising attorney reviews completed disbursement worksheets. Spot-check: fee calculation accuracy, expense verification, lien reconciliation, trust account notation.

**Level criteria**

| Level | Standard |
|-------|----------|
| **Trainee** | Shadow 3 disbursements, practice on 1 sample case, pass fee-calculation quiz |
| **Certified** | 10+ independent disbursements with <1 error per file, no trust account discrepancies |
| **Senior** | 50+ closures, mentors new billing staff, manages complex liens / subrogation cases |

## Supervising Attorney

**Purpose:** Provide legal judgment, approve gated actions, speak to clients on strategy (especially limits and settlement), supervise staff workflows, and ensure compliance with ethics rules.

**Required reading**

- [../system-spec.md](../system-spec.md) — full process map
- [09 — Demand prep checklist](../articles/09-demand-prep-checklist.md) — pre-demand review
- [10 — Settlement calculator scenarios](../articles/10-settlement-calculator-scenarios.md) — offer evaluation
- [07 — Thirty-day case review](../articles/07-thirty-day-case-review.md) — MRI / treatment strategy
- [37 — CLE & ethics compliance](../articles/37-cle-and-ethics-compliance.md)

**Competencies checklist**

- [ ] Review and approve demand packages before staff send (content, exhibits, strategy)
- [ ] Set lien reduction percentages or flat amounts per settlement negotiation
- [ ] Authorize final settlement acceptance and disbursement
- [ ] Approve drops and lien assertions (draft letter review + authority)
- [ ] Consult with clients on policy limits, treatment spend, and stop-treatment ramifications
- [ ] Conduct 30-day case reviews (MRI plan, outlier treatment, strategy calls)
- [ ] Approve 1P/MedPay/UM strategy timing (when to open, when to reserve)
- [ ] Review all staff-drafted letters implying legal conclusions or admissions
- [ ] Maintain audit trail of approvals and policy decisions per jurisdiction

**Assessment:** Partner / managing attorney spot-checks demand approvals, settlement authority log, and ethics compliance. Quarterly review of case strategy decisions.

**Level criteria**

| Level | Standard |
|-------|----------|
| **Trainee** | N/A — attorney role requires bar license + responsible attorney status |
| **Certified** | Authority to approve demands, settlements, and disbursements; maintains ethics compliance |
| **Senior** | Establishes firm strategy (settlement guidelines, fee policies), mentors junior attorneys |

## Firm Admin / Office Manager

**Purpose:** Stand up the firm on PraxiumLaw, invite staff with correct roles, enable workflows, and ensure training and disclosure compliance before live use.

**Required reading**

- [../SITE_WIRING_AUDIT.md](../SITE_WIRING_AUDIT.md) — what is wired vs PI spec
- [../DISCLOSURE.md](../DISCLOSURE.md) — white-label compliance
- [../WHITE_LABEL.md](../WHITE_LABEL.md) — template customization rules
- [38 — Firm operations & office policies](../articles/38-firm-operations-and-office-policies.md)
- [46 — Firm HIPAA & data security](../articles/46-firm-hipaa-and-data-security.md)

**Competencies checklist**

- [ ] Invite team members with correct RBAC roles (staff, paralegal, attorney, billing, admin)
- [ ] Customize all white-label templates with firm name, attorney bar numbers, address, fee %
- [ ] Have licensed counsel review and approve all templates before live use
- [ ] Enable intake and document workflows in Settings → Workflows
- [ ] Set up firm profile (name, jurisdictions, attorney roster, fee default)
- [ ] Manage public intake URL and client portal process documentation
- [ ] Implement security controls (JWT secrets, audit log restrictions, password gate until launch)
- [ ] Assign role guides to new hires and track training completion
- [ ] Maintain compliance documentation (counsel review dates, disclosure acknowledgments)

**Assessment:** Audit firm setup checklist completion. Partner / counsel confirm template customization and compliance. Spot-check team role assignments monthly.

**Level criteria**

| Level | Standard |
|-------|----------|
| **Trainee** | Completes go-live checklist under partner guidance; templates counsel-reviewed |
| **Certified** | Manages team invitations, workflow toggles, and quarterly template/disclosure reviews |
| **Senior** | Drives workflow optimization, coaches other firms on setup, leads security audits |

## Support Staff / Clerical

**Purpose:** Keep paper and digital files moving: scan/index documents, mail logistics, data entry support, calendar coordination, and client logistics — without legal advice or adjuster negotiations on injuries.

**Required reading**

- [18 — Document taxonomy](../articles/18-document-taxonomy.md)
- [01 — Intake needs list](../articles/01-intake-needs-list.md)
- [24 — Medical documents reference](../articles/24-medical-documents-reference.md)
- [34 — Correspondence & communications](../articles/34-correspondence-and-communications.md)

**Competencies checklist**

- [ ] Scan documents and upload to PraxiumLaw with correct taxonomy every time (no untagged files)
- [ ] Apply medical naming conventions (MR, B, FE, COR) on medical uploads
- [ ] Prepare certified mailing batches (addresses, tracking, logging in tasks)
- [ ] Index and organize physical files per firm SOP
- [ ] Coordinate calendar events and SOL reminders with case managers
- [ ] Collect client ID copies, declarations pages, insurance cards at intake
- [ ] Copy and fax documents per case manager direction (templates attorney-approved)
- [ ] Log expense receipts (police report, copy fees, certified mail costs)
- [ ] Support client portal uploads and document collection workflows

**Assessment:** Supervise first 20 document uploads. Spot-check: taxonomy accuracy, naming conventions, no missing labels. Monthly audit of physical file organization.

**Level criteria**

| Level | Standard |
|-------|----------|
| **Trainee** | 20+ supervised uploads, 0 taxonomy errors, passes document-naming quiz |
| **Certified** | 100+ unsupervised uploads, maintains file organization, handles mail logistics |
| **Senior** | Trains new clerical staff, optimizes scanning workflow, manages document archive |

## Virtual Assistant (Remote)

**Purpose:** Remote support for repeatable, checklist-driven tasks — data entry, document filing, appointment confirmation, records follow-up, and inbox hygiene — without legal advice or adjuster negotiations.

**Required reading**

- [01 — Intake needs list](../articles/01-intake-needs-list.md)
- [18 — Document taxonomy](../articles/18-document-taxonomy.md)
- [52 — Records retrieval & vendors](../articles/52-records-retrieval-and-vendors.md)
- [11 — Client call cadence](../articles/11-client-call-cadence.md)
- [../DISCLOSURE.md](../DISCLOSURE.md)

**Competencies checklist**

- [ ] Enter intake form data into PraxiumLaw (verify legibility with case manager first)
- [ ] Upload documents with correct taxonomy and naming (MR, B, FE, Intake)
- [ ] Confirm appointment dates and log reminders in tasks
- [ ] Call records vendors for status updates ("Checking on records request") — no medical advice
- [ ] Update contact information (adjuster phones, provider emails, facility fax)
- [ ] Triage lead inbox (flag hot leads, escalate to attorney/case manager)
- [ ] Log all client/adjuster contacts in matter Notes or tasks
- [ ] Review task queue daily and close completed items with notes
- [ ] Escalate angry clients, media inquiries, or legal questions immediately

**Assessment:** Case manager spot-checks data entry accuracy and document uploads. Review task completion notes weekly. Shadowing on first records call.

**Level criteria**

| Level | Standard |
|-------|----------|
| **Trainee** | 10 supervised uploads, 1 observed records call, passes data-entry accuracy check |
| **Certified** | 50+ independent uploads (0 taxonomy errors), 20+ records calls, task queue maintained daily |
| **Senior** | 200+ uploads, mentors new VAs, handles complex lead triage, multi-language capability |

## Renewal and advancement

- **Annual review:** Each staff member re-certifies on their role guide (updated procedures and disclosure).
- **Cross-training:** Certified staff may shadow adjacent roles (case manager → demand assistant → paralegal).
- **Edge cases:** Attorney approval required for staff to handle minors, complex liens, or multi-state matters regardless of certification.
