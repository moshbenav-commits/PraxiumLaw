Tettra Writing Guidelines

- In writing, write using a friendly, conversational
human, warm and helpful voice. 
- Please avoid using big words that people might not understand. 
Example: instead of using the word "utilize" just use the word "use".
- Break the piece into easy-to-follow sections with headings. 

Writing Suggestions
- You can write it here on Tettra or in Slack. 
- If you want to ask about a topic or concerns on a certain task, or even a suggestion to make us more organized, you can do it by making a new suggestion.
- A member of the team might know the answer to that or have that piece of information. 
So that person can do answer and would make it very easy for us to document things and not repeat the same question again and again. Helps us save a lot of time. :) 
- You can also choose the best person whom you know can best answer the question. 

Slack Integration
- You can search Tettra right from Slack as well. 
- Just link the channel from Slack to Tettra or vice versa. 
- /tettra find (name of page)
