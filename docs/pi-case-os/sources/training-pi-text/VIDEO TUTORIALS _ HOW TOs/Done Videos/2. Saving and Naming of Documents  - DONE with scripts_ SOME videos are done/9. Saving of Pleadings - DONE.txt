Saving of Pleadings and Discoveries

	•	A lawsuit begins when the person bringing the suit files a complaint. This first step begins what is known as the pleadings stage of the suit. Pleadings are certain formal documents filed with the court that state the parties' basic positions. Common pre-trial pleadings include: complaint, demand for jury trial, and summons.
	•	Discovery is a pre-trial procedure in a lawsuit in which each party, through the law of civil procedure, can obtain evidence from the other party or parties by means of discovery documents such as a request for answers to interrogatories, request for production of documents, request for admissions and depositions.
