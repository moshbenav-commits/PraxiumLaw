Saving Auto Insurance Documents

So let’s discuss each of the common insurance documents on how to save each of them.

We discuss on previous video the Acknowledgement of LOR, the other documents are:

	•	Denial or Acceptance of Liability. This letter states whether the 3p insurance accepts the liability or the fact that their insured is at fault. 
How to Save? Identify the client, insurance, type of document. Rename accordingly based on the information you have and on document naming convention. Save in auto insurance tab on its appropriate folder, check if the adjuster information has changed, if yes, update the adjuster information. Update the Liability Accepted field accordingly, and leave notes appropriately and upload the letter. Do not forget to click Save. Next is to post in feed by looking for an open task waiting for the letter you are about to save. Leave notes then attach the letter and click create. Last step is save in docs tab, and server.
	•	Declaration Page. This document contains the information on the available coverage, benefits, and their corresponding policy limits. 
How to Save? Identify the client, insurance, type of document. Rename accordingly based on the information you have and on document naming convention. Save in auto insurance tab and update the fields accordingly such as Commercial or Personal Policy, Policy Number, Policy Limits or UM UIM Limits, and Med pay Amount. Post in activity feed by looking first for an open task, if none, then post as a new feed. Check if there are hashtag of the policy limits, if yes compare with the listed info in Dec Page. If none, then add a hashtag of the policy limits on both primary and secondary cases. Last step is to save in docs tab, and in server.
	•	Repair Estimate/Total Loss or Market Valuation Report. Repair Estimate is the estimated costs of vehicle repair while Total Loss Valuation is when there is an extensive damage to a vehicle and the insurance company decides to declare the vehicle a total loss or “totaled.” This means that the cost to repair it to its pre-damaged state exceeds the cost of the vehicle's worth.
How to Save? Upload in Intake tab and update property damage related fields. Post in Activity Feed, look for an open task looking for a repair estimate. If none, post as a new feed. Then lastly, save in docs tab and in server. 
	•	Receipt of Demand/Demand Status Letter. This letter states whether they received the demand and on what process they are in prior they can provide a settlement offer. Some adjusters tell that our demand was sent to a Special Investigation Unit for further review, or sometimes, they are asking for past/prior medical records of client before they can arrive with the settlement offer. For instances like that, when there are letters that requires corresponding action, (like the adjuster is requesting for prior records), please ensure that you get this to the person who needs to work on it. Tag the CM and ask if you can or others can do the request. 
How to Save? Save in auto ins tab, and update the notes accordingly. If adjuster information had changed, please do update the adjuster or the adjuster contact card. Post in feed/task about the demand status. It is the feed where it was mentioned that demand was sent and is waiting for a response from the adjuster. Attach the letter,a nd please do leave notes accordingly to provide accurate and relevant information. Lastly, save in docs tab, and in server.
	•	Settlement Offer/Counter Offer. This letter provides the amount for an insurance company to settle the case. When an insurance gives a lowball offer, we do send counters for us to get a reasonable and fair offer. After that, they do send their counter offers, which is a response from our counter or request for a higher reasonable amount to settle.
How to Save? Save in auto insurance tab by leaving notes and attaching the document. After that, save in feed by posting on an open task that is waiting for the settlement offer, and inform the case managers by tagging them. I suggest to notify them via email by forwarding a copy of the settlement offer. Next is to update the Negotiations tab by updating the fields of type of offer, amount, date of offer, leaving notes, made by field, and leaving your name in ‘employee who relayed this offer’ field. Do not forget to attach the document. Lastly, save in docs tab, and in server.
	•	Release. This is a letter that is being received after agreeing to a settlement amount. This is being required by insurance companies before they issue the settlement check or  there are times when an insurance company send this letter along with the check. This letter memorializes the settlement amount and defines certain important terms related to the settlement and release of the claims related to the client’s accident.
How to Save? Save in auto insurance tab by attaching the document and leaving notes. After that, save in activity feed by posting on feed or an open task waiting for the release letter, task the CM in order for him/her to facilitate the signing of the release. Next is to save in docs tab, and in server.
	•	Checks (Med Pay Check, Property Damage Check, Settlement Check). This is the payment of client’s claims. A settlement check will indicate that the case was settled with the insurance companies and we will proceed in disbursement. 
How to Save? Save in feed by posting on an open task waiting for the check. Task our Bookkeeper (Janeen Moreno-Ibay) for her to check if posted in IOLTA account after 2 weeks, and tag the Case Manager. Save in trust account tab by adding an item, attaching the check, and updating these fields: ‘Payable to’ is to whom the check was addressed, date, amount, check#, and payment type. For Property Damage check with name of the client in it, no need to save in trust account tab, but to save it in intake tab, of course post in feed. Lastly, save these checks in docs tab and in server. 
Addition: Please forward the check to janeen@firm.example
	•	Indexing Forms and Medicare Request Forms. There are times when insurance companies send us forms to fill up regarding client’s information, treatment, and medicare information. Do not sign these forms, (unless tasked by CM) as we do not want to provide indexing information. When we do it, they will use these information to see if they will find any information such as prior accident or prior injuries a client might have from an accident, to lessen what they have to offer. Note: Only send a Medicare Form right after we settle or only when a CM instructed us to do so.
How to Save: Save in auto insurance tab, by attaching the document and leaving notes. Then, post in activity feed by attaching the documents, post if there is an open task asking for it. If none (which is most likely), post in new feed, and tag CM for her to be aware if it needs to be signed and sent back. Lastly, save in docs tab and in server.

There are other correspondence you may encounter but the basic rule is to save it in auto insurance tab and post in feed. If there are information and actions needed please ensure to inform the Case Manager or whomever needs to work on it. Also, do not forget to save in docs tab and server.
