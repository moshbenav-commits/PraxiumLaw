Drop Letters

These are letters we sent to inform the client, the insurance companies, and medical providers that we are no longer representing and dropping the client’s case.

What are the reasons why a case needs to be dropped?
	•	When the client is the one who is at fault
	•	When a client does not cooperate and is missing in action
	•	When there is a conflict of interest
	•	Client or a case is a fraud or a possible fraudClient retained another lawyer
	•	

How to draft?
	•	Go to specific tab where you need to send a drop letter.
	Client - Intake, Medical Provider - Meds tab, Auto Insurance - Insurance Auto tab
	•	Click generate and download. 
	•	After that edit the letter. Edit the highlighted fields in the letter.
	•	Send accordingly and update the task and  complete.
