Redaction of Personal Indexing Information

The process of redaction is to remove or to whiteout clients’ personal identifiable information. This is important for the reason that most insurance companies try and see if they have any information to lessen what they have to offer to our clients.  This might be a prior accident or prior injuries from an accident.  If they lower their settlement amount then we have to work harder and litigate the case which means that the client will have to wait longer to get their money.  Our cost will also increase because someone has not redacted private information.   

So what are the items we need to redact? These are date of birth, age, address, social security number, medicare ID number, vehicle identification number, plate number, health insurance information, sensitive health information such as STDs, or AIDS, ethnicity, and lien holder information.
