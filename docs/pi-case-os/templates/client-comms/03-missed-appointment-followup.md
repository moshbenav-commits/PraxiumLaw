# Missed Appointment Follow-Up

**When to use:** Sent shortly after a client misses or cancels a scheduled appointment, to check in without judgment and help them reschedule quickly (gaps in treatment can affect a case, so prompt rescheduling matters).

## EN

Hi {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, we noticed you weren't able to make it to your appointment with {{PROVIDER}} on {{DATE}}. No worries — these things happen.

Staying on track with your appointments is important, so let's get you rescheduled soon. Please call us at {{PHONE}} or reply here with a few times that work for you, and {{CASE_MANAGER}} will take care of the rest.

Coordination message — not legal advice.

## ES

Hola {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, notamos que no pudo asistir a su cita con {{PROVIDER}} el {{DATE}}. No se preocupe, estas cosas pasan.

Es importante mantener el ritmo de sus citas, así que reprogramemos pronto. Llámenos al {{PHONE}} o responda a este mensaje con algunos horarios que le convengan, y {{CASE_MANAGER}} se encargará del resto.

Mensaje de coordinación — no es asesoría legal.
