# Treatment Check-In

**When to use:** A periodic, low-pressure check-in during active treatment to ask how the client is doing and remind them the team is available if anything changes. Keep it general — no specific medical detail should be requested or referenced in the message itself.

## EN

Hi {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, just checking in — how have you been feeling with your treatment lately?

If anything has changed, if you're having trouble getting to appointments, or if you just have questions, {{CASE_MANAGER}} is here to help. You can reach us at {{PHONE}} anytime.

Coordination message — not legal advice.

## ES

Hola {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, le escribimos para saber cómo se ha sentido con su tratamiento últimamente.

Si algo ha cambiado, si tiene dificultades para asistir a sus citas, o si simplemente tiene preguntas, {{CASE_MANAGER}} está para ayudarle. Puede comunicarse con nosotros al {{PHONE}} en cualquier momento.

Mensaje de coordinación — no es asesoría legal.
