# Settlement Next Steps

**When to use:** When there is a development in the case that needs to be discussed by phone (e.g., a response from the carrier). This message must stay strictly neutral: it invites a call, and it never states, implies, or estimates an amount, outcome, or recommendation. Any figures or advice are discussed live, by the attorney, on the call — never in this message.

## EN

Hi {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, there's an update on your case that we'd like to review with you directly.

Could you give us a call at {{PHONE}} at your earliest convenience, or reply here with a good time to reach you? {{CASE_MANAGER}} will set up a time for you to speak with the team.

Coordination message — not legal advice.

## ES

Hola {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, hay una novedad en su caso que nos gustaría revisar con usted directamente.

¿Podría llamarnos al {{PHONE}} cuando le sea posible, o responder a este mensaje indicando un buen horario para contactarlo? {{CASE_MANAGER}} coordinará un momento para que hable con el equipo.

Mensaje de coordinación — no es asesoría legal.
