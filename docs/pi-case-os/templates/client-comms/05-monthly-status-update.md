# Monthly Status Update

**When to use:** A routine, recurring update (e.g., monthly) to let the client know their case is active and being worked, without giving legal advice or discussing case value. Good for periods when there's no major milestone to report.

## EN

Hi {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, this is your routine update from {{FIRM_NAME}} as of {{DATE}}.

Your case is active and {{CASE_MANAGER}} continues to work on it behind the scenes — following up on records, coordinating with providers, and keeping your file organized. There's nothing you need to do right now.

If your contact information, treatment, or availability changes, please let us know at {{PHONE}}.

Coordination message — not legal advice.

## ES

Hola {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, esta es su actualización de rutina de {{FIRM_NAME}} al {{DATE}}.

Su caso está activo y {{CASE_MANAGER}} continúa trabajando en él: dando seguimiento a los registros, coordinando con los proveedores y manteniendo su expediente organizado. Por ahora no necesita hacer nada.

Si su información de contacto, tratamiento o disponibilidad cambia, por favor avísenos al {{PHONE}}.

Mensaje de coordinación — no es asesoría legal.
