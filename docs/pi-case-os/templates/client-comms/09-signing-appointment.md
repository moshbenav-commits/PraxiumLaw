# Signing Appointment

**When to use:** To schedule an in-person or video appointment for the client to sign release/disbursement paperwork, and to let them know what to bring. No amounts or figures are referenced.

## EN

Hi {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, it's time to schedule your signing appointment. Here are the details:

- **Date:** {{DATE}}
- **Time:** {{TIME}}
- **Address:** {{ADDRESS}}

Please bring a valid photo ID. If you'd prefer a video appointment instead, or need to reschedule, call {{CASE_MANAGER}} at {{PHONE}}.

Coordination message — not legal advice.

## ES

Hola {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, es momento de coordinar su cita de firma. Estos son los detalles:

- **Fecha:** {{DATE}}
- **Hora:** {{TIME}}
- **Dirección:** {{ADDRESS}}

Por favor traiga una identificación con fotografía vigente. Si prefiere una cita por videollamada, o necesita reprogramar, llame a {{CASE_MANAGER}} al {{PHONE}}.

Mensaje de coordinación — no es asesoría legal.
