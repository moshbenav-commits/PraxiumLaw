# Appointment Reminder

**When to use:** Sent 1–2 days before a scheduled medical appointment to help the client remember the date, time, provider, and location.

## EN

Hi {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, this is a reminder about your upcoming appointment:

- **Date:** {{DATE}}
- **Time:** {{TIME}}
- **Provider:** {{PROVIDER}}
- **Address:** {{ADDRESS}}

If you need to reschedule, please call us at {{PHONE}} as soon as possible.

Coordination message — not legal advice.

## ES

Hola {{CLIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, le recordamos su próxima cita:

- **Fecha:** {{DATE}}
- **Hora:** {{TIME}}
- **Proveedor:** {{PROVIDER}}
- **Dirección:** {{ADDRESS}}

Si necesita reprogramar, por favor llámenos al {{PHONE}} lo antes posible.

Mensaje de coordinación — no es asesoría legal.
