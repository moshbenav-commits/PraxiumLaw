**Read this before using any PraxiumLaw materials, templates, scripts, or workflows.**

PraxiumLaw is a **case-management and operations toolkit**. It is **not** a law firm, **not** a substitute for a licensed attorney, and **not** legal advice for any jurisdiction or matter.

## White-label product

Materials are written for **any personal-injury law firm**. They do not endorse or affiliate with any particular firm. Historical training sources were used only to derive operational patterns; published product language is firm-neutral (`the firm`, `the attorney`, `the case manager`).

## You must edit documents and have counsel review language

Before using **any** letter, form, fee agreement, HIPAA authorization, demand, reduction request, intake packet, script, checklist, or workflow in a live matter:

1. **Customize** every document for your firm (letterhead, names, bar numbers, trust account language, fee percentages, jurisdiction).
2. **Have a licensed attorney in the relevant jurisdiction** review and approve the language.
3. **Confirm** statutes, ethics rules, contingent-fee rules, lien rules, and insurance practices for **your** state(s).
4. **Do not** assume Nevada or Washington examples apply elsewhere.
5. **Do not** send templates “as-is” to clients, carriers, providers, or courts.

Training examples may include fee splits, timelines, or coaching phrases that are **illustrative only**. Your firm’s policies and ethics obligations control.

## Attorney supervision

Staff workflows assume **attorney supervision**. Demand send, settlement authority, reduction percentages, treatment-spend approvals, and trust disbursements must remain under licensed attorney control as required by your jurisdiction.
