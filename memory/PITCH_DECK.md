2. **Native telephony** — own the stack; switching cost goes up over time
3. **Consumer brand (Praxa)** — direct relationship with end-users
4. **Marketplace network effects** — more firms ↔ more partner attorneys ↔ more consumers
5. **Voice cloning per attorney** — personalized clones are sticky
6. **Glass-Box AI audit trail** — regulatory-defensible by design

**Speaker note**: "Filevine is the obvious incumbent. They're $1B-valued but stagnant. The moat isn't features — it's the data network effect. Every closed case feeds our Settlement Comparables DB. Every consumer feeds our funnel. The longer we run, the more impossible it becomes to catch."
