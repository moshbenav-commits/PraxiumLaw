## DEMO SCRIPT (5 minutes)

### **MINUTE 1 — The Setup (60 seconds)**

> "Alright, I'm going to show you Praxium in five minutes. I'm not going to go deep on every module — I want you to see what makes this different from Filevine in the first 30 seconds."

**Action**: Sign in. Dashboard loads.

> "This is the firm dashboard. Six metrics at the top. Pipeline distribution across seven case stages. Recent activity feed. Standard stuff."
> 
> "But watch this."

**Action**: Press **⌘K** (Command Palette opens).

> "Anywhere in the app, ⌘K. Search across every matter, contact, note, document. Press a quick action — new matter, jump to MedConnect, claim a lead. Your hands never leave the keyboard."

**Action**: Type "Smith" → results appear → click into Smith matter.

> "I just opened the Smith v. Acme matter. ⌘K is what your team will use 50 times a day. Filevine doesn't have this."

### **MINUTE 2 — The Matter (60 seconds)**

**Action**: Matter detail loads with 7 tabs visible.

> "Every matter has the same structure. Overview here. Then seven tabs: tasks, documents, notes, MEDICAL, FILINGS, and CHAT. Most case management software stops at documents and notes. We have a chat thread per matter — every team conversation about Smith stays here forever. No more 'where did Sarah say that?'"

**Action**: Click Medical tab.

> "Medical tab. See this Patient ID? PT-A4F9K2. We auto-generate one for every client. Your client hands a printed card to their doctor. The doctor's office puts that ID in the subject line of any email or fax — we route it to this exact matter automatically. Records collection drops from 60 days to 18."

**Action**: Click back to Overview.

---

### **MINUTE 3 — CoCounsel AI (60 seconds) ← KEY MOMENT**

**Action**: Press **⌘J** (or click sparkles icon). CoCounsel sidebar slides in.

> "This is CoCounsel. Claude Sonnet 4.5 under the hood. It knows everything about Smith — the case description, the practice area, the incident date. Watch."

**Action**: Click "Summarize this matter" quick prompt.

> "Real-time streaming. Three seconds for a clean summary you could paste into a status email to your client."

**Action**: Wait for response to finish. Then type:

> "Draft a 30-day status update email I can send to the client."

**Action**: AI streams back a draft.

> "This isn't a chatbot. This is a colleague that's read every file. Glass-Box transparency — you can see every prompt, every response, every source. Audit-friendly. Filevine's Sidebar AI is rated 2 out of 5 on G2 because it's a black box that hallucinates. Ours is verifiable."

**Action**: Close sidebar with ⌘J.

**Action**: Click on a lead → claim → convert. New matter appears.

## Q&A — MOST COMMON QUESTIONS

### "How long does migration actually take?"
> "14 days from signed agreement to cutover. Mirror Mode runs Filevine alongside Praxium for 30 days so you never lose data. By Day 30, you cancel Filevine and the savings start immediately. We do the data import. You do nothing."

### "What about the consultants we paid to set up Filevine?"
> "Praxium has no consultants. Practice-area packs apply automatically based on what we detect from your data. PI firm? You get PI workflows on Day 1. We do this in 30 seconds, not 30 days."

### "What about our trust accounting?"
> "Three-way reconciliation is built in. We import your existing IOLTA history with the original ledger chain. Bar examiners love us because the audit trail is cryptographically tied. (Phase 2 if not yet live — be honest about timing.)"

### "Filevine just announced [new feature X]. What about that?"
> "Two answers. First — we usually ship within 90 days when something becomes real demand. Second — feature parity is the wrong frame. Filevine charges $500-$2000/month extra for that. We bundle. The economics break before the features do."

### "What if the AI makes a mistake?"
> "Two layers. First — Glass-Box AI. Every action is auditable, sources cited. Second — our system prompts include 'do not hallucinate; if unsure, say so.' Practical answer: it's a copilot, not an autopilot. Your attorneys still review. But it cuts an hour of drafting to ten minutes."

### "What about HIPAA?"
> "Encryption at rest + in transit, BAA available, audit logs per-record, scope-isolated provider portals. Hardening completes Q1 2027 with formal SOC2. If you need a BAA today, we sign one. (Be honest if not yet — say 'on the roadmap, Q1.')"

### "How does the marketplace make us money?"
> "Two ways. One — Marketplace-tier subscribers get leads delivered. You're paying $299/user for the subscription, then we send qualified leads — average $150 per claimed lead, but you only pay when you convert. Two — case results that close in our system feed your firm's reputation in the Praxa app. More cases over time."

### "What if I just want practice management — no AI, no marketplace?"
> "Starter tier — $99/user. Strips out the marketplace and most of the heavy AI. Still includes matters, contacts, docs, tasks, calendar, basic CoCounsel, e-sign, intake forms, reports. Most firms outgrow Starter within 6 months and upgrade — but you don't have to."

### "Is my data safe if I cancel?"
> "Always. You can export everything you put in within 24 hours. No hostage situation. We don't have your data — you do."

### "Who else is using this?"
> "[Pilot firm names if you have permission]. Happy to introduce you to anyone you want to talk to."
> 
> *(If pre-revenue: "We're in design-partner phase with 3 firms. I can connect you with them if you'd like to verify before signing.")*

## RED FLAGS — WHEN TO WALK AWAY

These signals mean this firm isn't a good fit (yet):
- Solo practitioner with <10 matters total → too small for our pricing
- Firm with multiple offices in different time zones → wait for Phase 3 multi-office
- Defense-side insurance firm → we're plaintiff-focused; not optimized for them yet
- BigLaw (>100 attorneys) → enterprise pricing conversation, separate sales process
- Firm that just signed a 3-year Filevine contract within last 6 months → too painful to extract right now, revisit Year 2
