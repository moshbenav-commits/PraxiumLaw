## TARGETING

**Best targets**:
- PI firms with 5–30 attorneys
- Currently on Filevine (LinkedIn job posts mentioning "Filevine experience" = signal)
- 3+ years on Filevine (past honeymoon phase, feeling pain)
- Recent G2/Capterra reviewer with <4 stars (high-intent unhappy)

**Worst targets (skip)**:
- Solo practitioners on Clio Manage (too small, different price band)
- Firms <12 months into Filevine (still inside honeymoon)
- BigLaw (different procurement; uses Aderant/Elite)

## RESPONSE-HANDLING PLAYBOOK

### "We're happy with Filevine"
→ "Totally fair. Out of curiosity — what would have to be true for you to consider switching in 24 months? I'll check back then."  
*(Plant the seed. Most "we're happy" replies become "we're not happy" within 18 months.)*

### "We're locked into a contract"
→ "Most Filevine contracts are 12-month. We routinely credit firms for their remaining months — up to $20k — so the switch is net-zero on day one. When does yours renew?"

### "Send me a deck"
→ Send the 1-pager (not the full deck). Then: "Happy to walk through this on a 15-min call. What's a good day next week?"

### "Send me pricing"
→ "Easy: $199/user/month for Pro tier (where most firms land). 15 users = $2,985/mo. Free migration. 90-day money back. Want me to calc your specific savings vs current stack?"

### "I'll think about it"
→ "Of course. To help your thinking — what's the ONE thing your current setup does that you're worried we won't replace? Let me address that directly."

### "Not interested"
→ "Got it. I'll stop reaching out. If anything changes — even 2 years from now — text me directly: [phone]. Best of luck."
